Who we are

An independent service, not a power company

BijleeBill.pk is an independent bill-checking convenience service built for Pakistani electricity consumers. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to PITC, any DISCO, K-Electric or the Government of Pakistan.

The idea came from a simple frustration. Paper bills get lost. Official sites are slow on phones. And every company keeps its bill page in a different corner of the internet. We put one clean front door in front of all of them.

What we do

One front door to the official bill pages

You pick your company and enter your reference number. We send it straight to the official portal. That is bill.pitc.com.pk for the PITC companies. Or the official K-Electric website for Karachi. Your bill opens from the company's own system.

Around that simple flow we built a bill calculator with real NEPRA slabs and charges, plus a growing library of practical guides. Every guide is written from official sources and first-hand portal testing.

We never hold your bill. The reference number travels from your browser to the official portal. Any number you save stays in your own browser storage.

How we earn

Free for you, paid by display ads

The service is and stays free. We earn through unobtrusive display advertising served by Google AdSense. We do not sell personal data. We do not charge for bill checks. And ads never stand between you and your bill.

That is the whole business model. If it ever changes, this page changes with it.

Our standards

Official sources or nothing

Every route we publish points to an official portal. Every fact in our guides is checked against company websites, NEPRA documents or the live behaviour of the portals themselves. When we cannot verify something, we leave it out.

Found a mistake? Write to support@bijleebill.pk and we will fix it fast. Corrections are welcome, not embarrassing.
