In short

We do not collect your personal data

BijleeBill.pk has no accounts, no sign-ups and no server-side storage of your bills. This policy explains the few things that do happen, in plain words.

Information

What happens to the data you type

When you check a bill, your reference number is sent from your browser. It goes directly to the official portal, for example bill.pitc.com.pk. It does not pass through or sit on our servers.

If you use the save feature, the number stays in your own browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes it. We cannot read it from anywhere.

We never ask for your name, CNIC, email or payment details. If you email us yourself, we keep that message only to answer you.

Cookies and ads

Cookies, Google AdSense and your choices

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. This site uses minimal cookies only where needed for basic functionality.

We show display advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google uses advertising cookies. They let it and its partners serve ads to you. Those ads are based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Log data

What our hosting records

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard log data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. This data supports security and basic traffic statistics. It is not linked to any profile of you.

Children

Not designed for children

The service is intended for adult bill payers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes

We may update this policy as the site grows. Every change lands on this page with a new revision date below. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.

Questions

Talk to us

Any privacy question goes to support@bijleebill.pk. We answer within two to three working days.
