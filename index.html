Questions

Electricity bill checking FAQ

Where do I find my reference number?

It is usually printed near the top-left of your electricity bill. PITC companies use a 14-digit number; K-Electric uses a 13-digit account number.

Is this the official electricity bill website?

No. BijleeBill.pk is an independent convenience service that sends you directly to official public portals such as bill.pitc.com.pk and ke.com.pk.

Can I pay the bill here?

No payment is collected here. After checking the amount, use your bank app, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or an official DISCO payment channel.
