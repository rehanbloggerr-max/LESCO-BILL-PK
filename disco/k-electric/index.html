K-Electric bill online: what it shows and how to pay it

Your K-Electric bill is the official monthly statement for your Karachi connection. KE runs its own billing system rather than the PITC portal, and its official page opens from our checker with the same flow: enter the number, read the bill, save the copy.

Where K-Electric supplies power

K-Electric serves Karachi and its surrounding districts, the country's biggest single city load. Homes, shops, high-rises and industrial estates all read the same KE bill layout, with its own tariff names and peak/off-peak blocks.

The numbers on a KE bill

KE uses a 13-digit account number instead of the WAPDA-style 14-digit reference. It is printed prominently on the bill and is the only key the official KE page asks for. There is no separate consumer number field on that portal.

Paying and keeping records

Pay the KE bill through bank apps, banking counters, JazzCash or Easypaisa using the 13-digit account number. Save the PDF before paying and file the receipt with it. The paid status can take a day or two to update.

Paper bill missing in Karachi? The bill-not-received guide walks through the fixes, KE included.

Is the K-Electric online bill valid for payment?

Yes. The online copy mirrors the paper bill and every normal payment channel accepts the account number.

Which number do I enter for a KE bill check?

The 13-digit account number printed on your KE bill. KE does not use the 14-digit WAPDA reference.

I paid my KE bill but it still shows unpaid. Why?

Status updates can take 24 to 48 hours. Keep the receipt and recheck the next day.
