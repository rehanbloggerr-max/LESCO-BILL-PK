About GEPCO bill checking

Gujranwala Electric Power Co. (GEPCO) provides electricity in the Gujranwala region. Common service areas include Gujranwala, Sialkot, Gujrat, Narowal and nearby sub-divisions. If the official page is slow on a peak billing day, try again after a few minutes or check from another browser.

Can I check the bill without the number?

Some companies support a customer ID, otherwise find an old bill, SMS, payment receipt, or contact the official helpline.

Can I pay after viewing the bill?

Yes. Use the due amount from the official page and pay through your bank app, JazzCash, Easypaisa, ATM, or official office.

Is my number saved?

The interactive checker stores only your recent numbers in your own browser for convenience. BijleeBill.pk servers do not receive or log them.
