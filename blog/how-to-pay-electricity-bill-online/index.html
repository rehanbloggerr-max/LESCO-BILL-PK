Before you start

Before you pay, check the bill first

It sounds obvious, but many mistakes start with skipping the bill check. Always verify the current invoice before paying. This helps you confirm the month, amount, and account being charged.

Keep your reference number or customer ID ready and make sure you are paying the correct DISCO for your connection.

Best habit: check the latest bill online, compare it with the amount shown in your payment app, then proceed only if both match.
Payment options

Common ways to pay electricity bill online

Pakistani users commonly pay through the channels below. Choose the one you already trust and where you can review the final amount clearly.

  • Mobile banking apps
  • Wallet apps and payment platforms
  • Internet banking portals
  • ATM or branch-based digital payment options
  • Official or supported payment gateways
Pick consistency: use the same trusted method every month so you can easily compare receipts and spot changes.
Step by step

How to pay your bill online

  1. Open your payment appGo to the bill payment or utility payment section.
  2. Select your electricity companyChoose the correct DISCO, such as LESCO, IESCO, or another provider.
  3. Enter your reference numberPaste the exact 14-digit number or customer ID.
  4. Review the amountConfirm the bill month, payable amount, and any displayed charges.
  5. Complete paymentApprove the transaction and keep the receipt or transaction ID.

Safety

Safety checks to remember

Online bill payments are convenient, but a few small checks go a long way. Never rush through the confirmation screen, especially when the amount looks unusual.

  • Confirm the bill month and amount before paying.
  • Double-check the reference number for your connection.
  • Avoid paying through unknown or suspicious links.
  • Save the receipt or screenshot for your records.
  • Use your own device and network when possible.
Important: if a payment page shows a completely different name or amount, stop and verify the reference number again.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay someone else’s bill online?

Yes, but make sure you have their exact reference number and verify the amount before paying.

What if I pay the wrong account?

Contact your payment provider immediately and keep the receipt ready for support.

Is it better to pay near the due date?

It is safer to pay a few days earlier so any delay or maintenance issue does not cause a late fee.
