Bijli ka bill check karna mushkil nahi hai. Lekin aksar log internet ki wajah se atak jate hain. Portal khulta nahi. Data khatam ho jata hai aur English samajh nahi aati. Phir bill reh jata hai.

Ye guide alag hai. Hum yahan portal par click karne ka tareeqa nahi batayenge. Wo sab English guide mein likha hai. Yahan sirf woh tareeqe hain jo aam phone par chalte hain. SMS se. Call se, ya bina internet ke.

Har tareeqe ke sath hum ye bhi batayenge ke kaunsi baat official hai, aur kaunsi sirf logon ke kehne par. Ye farq zaroori hai. Ghalat number par SMS bhejne se paisay kat-te hain, bill nahi milta.

Aap chahe Lahore mein ho, Karachi mein, ya kisi chhote shehar mein. Ye tareeqe pooray Pakistan mein kaam karte hain. Bas apna reference number sath rakhein.

Quick answer

Sab se tez tareeqa kya hai

SMS sab se tez hai. Apne phone ka message app kholein. 'pitc' likhein. Ek space dein. Phir apna 14 digit reference number likhein. Ab is message ko 8334 par bhej dein.

Jawab mein aap ko bill ki raqam mil jayegi. Aakhri tareekh bhi milegi. Units bhi likhe hote hain. Ye sab kuch chand seconds mein aa jata hai.

Ye tareeqa har network par chalta hai. Jazz ho, Telenor, Zong, ya Ufone. Smartphone zaroori nahi. Kisi bhi purane phone se SMS ja sakta hai.

Ek baat yaad rakhein. 8334 wala number kayi websites ne likha hai aur hum ne is ki official tasdeeq nahi kar saki. Is liye pehle ek dafa aazma kar dekhein. Jawab na aaye to 118 par call karein.

Pehle ye

Shuru karne se pehle kya chahiye

Bill check karne ke liye sirf do cheezein chahiye. Ek to apna reference number. Doosra ek phone jis se SMS ya call ho sake.

Reference number 14 digits ka hota hai aur ye har purane bill par chhapa hota hai. Aksar upar ki taraf, naam ke paas. Is ke aakhir mein kabhi 'U' ya 'R' bhi likha hota hai.

Customer ID bhi kaam aa jata hai aur ye chhota number hota hai. LESCO isay 7 digit ka batata hai. Kuch jagah 10 digit likha milta hai.

Bahut log CNIC se bill dhoondhne ki koshish karte hain. Wo kaam nahi karta. Naam se bhi nahi milta aur meter number se bhi nahi. Sirf reference number ya customer ID chalta hai.

Agar aap ke paas koi purana bill nahi hai, to ghabrayein nahi. Neeche ek section hai jisme hum ne bataya hai ke number kahan se mil sakta hai.

SMS se

SMS se bijli ka bill kaise check karein

Ye tareeqa sab se pehle aazmayein aur is mein internet ki zaroorat nahi. Sirf SMS chalna chahiye.

  1. Message app kholein. Apne phone mein SMS ka option dhoondhein.
  2. 'pitc' likhein. Chhote ya bade harf, dono chalte hain, is se koi farq nahi parta.
  3. Space. Lafz ke baad ek khali jagah zaroor chhorein.
  4. Apna poora 14 digit reference number yahan likhein. Das ya baara digit se kaam nahi chalega.
  5. Ab is message ko 8334 par bhej dein. Network ke hisaab se charge katega.
  6. Intezar karein.
  7. Chand second mein raqam aa jati hai. Isay screenshot kar lein.

Ek misaal samajh lein. Aap ka reference number 04123456789012 hai. To message kuch yun hoga: pitc 04123456789012. Bas itna.

Jawab mein aap ko raqam milegi. Aakhri tareekh milegi. Kabhi units bhi likhe hote hain. Is message ko screenshot kar lein ya likh lein.

Har SMS par charge lag sakta hai aur apne network ke rates check kar lein. Ye bill check ki fee nahi hai. Ye aam SMS ka charge hai.

Agar pehle SMS se jawab na aaye, to dobara koshish karein. Kabhi network busy hota hai. Kabhi number mein ghalti ho jati hai.

Masla aaye

SMS ka jawab na aaye to kya karein

Kabhi SMS bhejne ke baad kuch nahi aata. Ye aam baat hai. Ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi. Neeche har wajah aur us ka hal likha hai.

Number mein ghalti

Aksar masla yahin hota hai. 14 digits theek likhein aur space 'pitc' ke baad zaroor dein. Das ya baara digit likhne se kaam nahi chalega.

Network busy

Kabhi network mein load hota hai aur sMS late jata hai. Das minute baad dobara koshish karein. Raat ke waqt aksar tez jawab milta hai.

Ghalat shortcode

Kayi websites alag alag number likhti hain aur 8334 sab se zyada bataya gaya hai. 8118 bhi kuch jagah likha hai. Dono aazma kar dekhein.

Balance nahi

SMS ke liye balance chahiye hota hai. Apna balance check karein. Prepaid users ko ye masla aksar hota hai.

Signal kamzor

Kam signal mein SMS jata nahi. Khuli jagah par jayein. Ya ghar ke kisi aur kamre se koshish karein.

Agar sab kuch theek hai aur phir bhi jawab nahi, to agla tareeqa aazmayein. Phone call sab se pakka hai.

Call se

Phone call se bijli ka bill kaise check karein

SMS se kaam na bane to call karein aur ye tareeqa sab se bharosay ka hai. Aap seedha insaan se baat karte hain.

Call karne se pehle apna reference number sath rakhein. Operator sab se pehle yahi poochhega. Number na ho to wo account dhoondh nahi payega.

Call karte waqt apna naam aur pata bhi batana parega. Kabhi CNIC bhi poochh lete hain. Ye sab verification ke liye hota hai.

Call ka charge aam call jitna hai. Koi extra fee nahi. 118 par call karna muft ya sasta hota hai.

Ek baat batate chalein. MEPCO apni site par 118 nahi likhta. Wo apna mobile number deta hai aur is liye upar di gayi table zaroor dekhein.

Number kho gaya

Reference number kahan se milega

Bill ghum ho gaya. Ab aap ko number kahan se milega? Ye sab se aam sawal hai. Kayi tareeqe hain.

Purani SMS

Aksar company har mahine SMS bhejti hai aur us mein number likha hota hai. Apne phone ka inbox check karein.

JazzCash ya Easypaisa

Agar aap ne kabhi bill in apps se bhara hai, to wahan record hoga. Transaction history kholein. Number wahan likha milega.

Bank ki receipt

Bank se bill bhara ho to receipt sambhal kar rakhein. Us par reference number chhapa hota hai. Purani receipt bhi kaam aa jati hai.

Helpline par call

118 par call karein. Apna naam, pata, aur CNIC batayein. Operator aap ka number dhoondh kar batayega.

Sub-division office

Sab se aakhiri tareeqa hai aur apne area ke bijli office jayein. Apna CNIC sath le jayein. Wahan se number mil jayega.

Number mil jaye to foran apne phone mein save kar lein. Ek contact bana lein. Aage har mahine kaam aayega.

Karachi

Karachi walon ke liye alag tareeqa

Karachi mein K-Electric bijli deti hai aur ye baqi Pakistan se alag hai. Is ka apna system hai.

  1. 13 digit account number dhoondhein. Ye har KE bill par chhapa hota hai.
  2. 118 par call karein. KE ka contact centre chaubees ghante khula rehta hai, raat ho ya din.
  3. Number batayein. Operator ko ye number dein.
  4. Bill ki tafseel sun lein. Raqam, aakhri tareekh, aur units sab note kar lein, kagaz par ya phone mein.
  5. QR code check karein. Bill ke 'Message Board' par QR hota hai.
  6. Apna mobile number bhi register karwa lein. Phir har mahine SMS aa jayega.

KE ka 14 digit reference number nahi hota aur wo 13 digit account number use karta hai. Ye baqi companies se farq hai.

KE apne bill par QR code chhapta hai. Isay scan kar ke subsidy ka registration ho jata hai. Ye sarkari program hai.

Baqi sab kuch same hai aur duplicate bill muft hai. Call muft hai. Koi jaldi ki fee nahi.

Bina phone

Bina phone ke bill kaise dekhein

Sab ke paas phone nahi hota aur kuch log internet use nahi karte. Un ke liye bhi raaste hain.

  • Sub-division office jayein. Apna purana bill ya CNIC sath le jayein.
  • Bank branch jayein. Counter par reference number dein aur printed copy lein, jo har jagah qabool hoti hai.
  • Easypaisa ya JazzCash shop. Number dein.
  • Kisi bijli payment wali dukan se poochhein. Wo bill nikal dete hain.
  • Apne mohalle ke electrician se poochhein. Aksar un ke paas purane bill hote hain.
  • Library ya internet cafe. Wahan baith kar portal kholein.

Har tareeqe mein reference number chahiye aur ye number yaad rakhein ya kahin likh lein. Ye sab se zaroori cheez hai.

Office jane se pehle timings check kar lein. Aksar subah 8 se shaam 3 tak khula rehta hai. Zayada waqt do din chhutti hoti hai.

Bank branch mein thoda waqt lagta hai aur lekin wahan printed copy mil jati hai. Wo copy har jagah qabool hoti hai.

Data ki bachat

Internet use karein to kitna data lagta hai

SMS sab se kam data leta hai. Lekin kabhi portal kholna hi parta hai. Tab data ki bachat ka ye tareeqa kaam aata hai.

Pehle apna reference number copy kar lein. Phir portal kholein aur number paste karein. Search dabayein. Is mein kam data lagta hai.

Portal ki tasveeray load hone dete hain to data zyada lagta hai. Browser mein images band kar dein. Sirf text load hoga.

Ek baar bill mil jaye to screenshot le lein. Baar baar portal kholne ki zaroorat nahi. Screenshot se kaam chal jata hai.

Agar ghar mein wifi hai, to wifi par portal kholein. Mobile data bach jayega. Ye sab se aasan tareeqa hai.

Ghalt fehmiyan

Log kya ghalat samajhte hain

Bill check karne ke bare mein kayi ghalt baatein phaili hain. Har ek ko saaf karte hain. Kisi ka naam nahi leinge.

CNIC se bill mil jata hai

Nahi milta. PITC ka system sirf reference number ya customer ID se dhoondhta hai. CNIC, naam, aur pata is mein kaam nahi karte.

Har company ka alag helpline hai

Poori tarah sahi nahi. 118 IESCO aur K-Electric dono ka hai aur lekin MEPCO apna alag mobile number deta hai. Ek hi number sab ke liye nahi.

SMS sirf smartphone par chalta hai

Ghalat. SMS kisi bhi phone se jata hai aur chahe wo purana button wala phone ho. Internet ki zaroorat hi nahi.

Duplicate bill ke paise lagte hain

Bilkul nahi. Duplicate bill muft hai. Jo bhi aap se paisay mangay, wo theek nahi. Bill check karna hamesha muft hai.

Internet zaroori hai

Nahi. SMS se kaam chal jata hai. Phone call se bhi ho jata hai aur office ja kar bhi ho jata hai.

Raat ko service band rehti hai

K-Electric ka contact centre 24 ghante chalta hai. SMS service bhi aksar chalti rehti hai. Sirf office timings fixed hain.

In mein se koi bhi baat sunayein to pehle khud check karein. Aksar log purani baatein dohrate hain.

Asli sarkari sites hamesha .pk par khatam hoti hain. Aksar .com.pk ya .gov.pk. Koi bhi site jo .com ya .net ho, wo sarkari nahi.

Koi bhi site aap se card ka number na mangay. Bill check ke liye card ki zaroorat nahi. OTP bhi nahi. Sirf reference number chahiye.

Shikayat

Bill theek na ho to shikayat kahan karein

Bill ghalat aaya ho. Ya raqam zyada likhi ho. To shikayat ka sahi tareeqa ye hai.

  1. Reference number sath rakhein. Is ke baghair shikayat register nahi hoti.
  2. ccms.pitc.com.pk kholein. Ye sarkari complaint portal hai, koi account banana nahi parta.
  3. Apna number darj karein. Reference number aur mobile number dono dena hoga.
  4. OTP confirm karein. Mobile par aaya code yahan dalen.
  5. 'Bill Correction' ka option chunein. Category ki list mein se yahi chunein.
  6. Jo complaint number milay, isay sambhal lein. Aage status check karne ke kaam aayega.

Shikayat register karne ke baad ek number milta hai. Is number se aap status check kar sakte hain. Kabhi call bhi aati hai.

Agar online masla ho, to 118 par call karein. Operator shikayat register kar dega. Ya 8118 par SMS bhej dein.

Complaint portal mein koi account banana nahi parta. Koi registration nahi. Sirf number aur OTP aur ye sab muft hai.

Zaban

Portal English mein hai to kya karein

Sare sarkari portals English mein hain. Ye baat kayi logon ke liye mushkil hai. Kuch aasan tareeqe hain.

Pehla tareeqa ye hai ke SMS use karein. SMS mein koi English samajhne ki zaroorat nahi. Sirf 'pitc' aur number. Bas.

Doosra tareeqa phone call hai. Operator se apni zaban mein baat kar lein. Aksar Urdu mein jawab mil jata hai.

Teesra tareeqa ye hai ke kisi ghar ke bacche se madad lein. Chhote bacche aksar English portal asani se chala lete hain.

Chautha tareeqa office jana hai. Wahan ka staff aap ki zaban mein samjhayega. Thoda waqt lagta hai, lekin kaam ho jata hai.

Ye guide bhi is liye Roman Urdu mein hai. Taake aap ko kisi aur ki madad ki zaroorat na pare.

Kam resources

Kam battery ya kam signal mein kya karein

Kabhi phone ki battery khatam hoti hai. Kabhi signal nahi aata. Dono halat mein kuch tareeqe kaam aate hain.

Battery kam ho

SMS call se kam battery leta hai. Internet band kar dein. Screen ki roshni kam kar dein. Phir SMS bhejein.

Signal kamzor ho

Khuli jagah par jayein. Chhat ya bahar ki taraf aur sMS ko chand minute lag sakte hain. Bar bar dobara na bhejein.

Internet slow ho

Portal kholne ki koshish na karein. SMS bhej dein. Ye slow internet par bhi chalta hai.

Flight mode chalu ho

Pehle flight mode band karein. Aksar log bhool jate hain aur is wajah se SMS jata hi nahi.

Phone purana ho

Purane phone par SMS sab se behtar hai. Internet ki setting ki zaroorat nahi. Bas number aur message.

Ye chhoti baatein hain. Lekin inhi se kaam banta hai. Aksar masla phone ka nahi, setting ka hota hai.

Sawalat

Aksar poochhe gaye sawal

Bijli ka bill SMS se kaise check karein?

Apne phone se SMS likhein. 'pitc' likhein, ek space dein, phir apna 14 digit reference number. Is message ko 8334 par bhej dein. Jawab mein bill ki raqam aur aakhri tareekh mil jayegi. Ye tareeqa har network par chalta hai.

Bina internet ke bijli ka bill kaise dekhein?

SMS se ya phone call se. SMS ke liye 'pitc' likhein, space dein, reference number likhein, aur 8334 par bhejein. Call ke liye 118 number hai. Dono tareeqon mein internet ki zaroorat nahi.

Bijli bill check karne ka kya charge hai?

Duplicate bill check karna bilkul muft hai. Koi fee nahi. Sirf aap ke network ka aam SMS charge lag sakta hai. Jo site aap se bill check ke paisay mangay, wo sahi nahi.

Reference number bhool gaye to kya karein?

Kayi tareeqe hain. Purani SMS check karein aur jazzCash ya Easypaisa ki history dekhein. Bank ki receipt dekhein. Ya 118 par call kar ke apna naam aur pata batayein. Operator number dhoondh dega.

CNIC se bijli ka bill check ho sakta hai?

Nahi. PITC ka system sirf reference number ya customer ID se dhoondhta hai. CNIC, naam, aur meter number se bill check nahi hota. Ye baat kai logon ko maloom nahi hoti.

K-Electric ka bill kaise check karein?

K-Electric ke liye 13 digit account number chahiye aur ye har KE bill par hota hai. Is ke sath 118 par call karein. KE ka contact centre 24 ghante khula rehta hai.

Bijli bill ki complaint kahan karein?

ccms.pitc.com.pk par jayein. Ye sarkari complaint portal hai. Apna reference number aur mobile number dein. OTP confirm karein. 'Bill Correction' chunein. Ya 118 par call kar dein.

Bill milne ke baad details phir check karein
