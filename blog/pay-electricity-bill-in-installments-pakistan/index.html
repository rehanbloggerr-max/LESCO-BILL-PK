What this guide can and cannot promise

A large electricity bill can land at a bad time. Installments may spread the cost. Approval is not automatic.

Searches for "pay electricity bill in installments Pakistan" should lead first to your DISCO's current rules.

NEPRA's revised 2025 Consumer Service Manual says a DISCO may offer installments under its business requirements. Each company sets its own process.

This guide separates current-month bills from old arrears. It also gives you questions to ask before agreeing to a schedule. No promise of approval here.

Use your own company's official website, app or customer service channel. Do not trust posts promising one officer limit for every DISCO.

Quick answer

Quick answer

To pay electricity bill in installments Pakistan, ask your DISCO about your current bill or old arrears. The company may approve or refuse the request.

For an approved current-bill plan, pay the first installment by the due date. That payment avoids markup and LPS. Later installments carry 14% annual pro-rata markup.

Get the computerized revised bill before paying. NEPRA's Legal page lists the revised 2025 manual and clause 6.8. A PESCO-hosted copy is also available.

Current bill

Current-month bill installments

Clause 6.8.2 covers requests for installments on the current month's bill. The first payment has a specific rule.

Pay it by the bill's due date. The manual says the first installment carries no markup or late payment surcharge under this condition.

Later installments carry markup at 14% per year, pro rata. The DISCO still decides whether to offer the plan.

Ask for the amount, dates and markup calculation before agreeing. The manual does not set one national installment count.

Old arrears

Installments for old arrears

Old arrears are not automatically covered by the current-bill rule. Ask your DISCO about its local policy.

IESCO's official FAQ says installments are not available on a running bill there. It says old arrears may be paid in installments, with interest at prevailing bank rates.

The FAQ also lists local eligibility conditions and a written undertaking. Its revision date is unclear. Check the terms with IESCO before relying on that page.

Ask whether new electricity use must be paid alongside the arrears plan. Get the schedule for both amounts.

Approval

Who approves the request?

Clause 6.8.3 says DISCOs may offer installments under their business requirements. It does not guarantee approval. It also names no single officer for every company.

Some older local pages list officer limits. Dates matter. Ask your own DISCO for its current approval route and policy.

Ask your DISCO for the correct office and approving officer. Request a written copy of the current policy name or its official link for your records. Keep the answer with your bill.

A social post may promise a fixed installment count. Check such claims with care. The current manual leaves availability to each DISCO.

Markup

Markup and late payment surcharge

For a current-month plan under clause 6.8.2, the first installment and later payments follow different rules.

Pay the first installment by the due date. The manual offers a clear break here: no markup or LPS applies to the first payment. Remaining installments carry markup at 14% per year, pro rata.

This is not a flat 14% charge on the full bill. That figure is an annual rate. It applies pro rata to remaining installments. Ask the DISCO to show the calculation on your revised bill.

Arrears may follow local rules. IESCO's FAQ, for example, refers to prevailing bank rates. Do not apply the current-bill rate to arrears without confirmation.

Due date

Due-date extension is a separate request

An installment plan and a due-date extension are different requests. Ask the DISCO: Is this an extension or an installment plan?

Clause 6.8.1 says an approved due-date extension prevents LPS. An unanswered request does not change the date on your bill.

Keep to the printed due date until the DISCO confirms an extension or issues a revised bill. Time is short? Use the official contact route listed by your company.

A phone promise is hard to prove later. Get written or app confirmation. Save its reference number or screenshot.

Revised bill

Read the computerized revised bill

Clause 6.8.4 says the DISCO generates a computerized bill after it allows installments or a due-date extension.

Pause before paying. Match the consumer reference and bill month. Inspect the approved amount and first payment. Note each later date.

Look for markup or LPS. Ask the DISCO to explain any unclear calculation. Keep the revised bill and each receipt.

Do not pay a handwritten amount to a person or account the DISCO has not confirmed. Use a payment channel shown by your company.

Prepare

Prepare your request

Keep your current bill nearby. Note the reference number, consumer name, bill month, total amount and due date.

For a request to pay electricity bill in installments Pakistan, keep your latest bill and reference number ready.

These details help staff find the account. They are not a national list of required documents. Ask your DISCO about any ID, form or written request it needs.

Decide what you need to ask. Is this for the current bill or old arrears? Can you pay the first amount by the due date? What schedule can you manage?

Send only the details requested by the official channel. Keep a copy and the submission reference.

Application route

Where to apply

The CSM does not prescribe one nationwide application form for electricity bill installments. Filing routes differ between DISCOs.

Start with the customer-service contact or official app listed by your company. Some requests may go through a subdivision or customer service centre. Others may use a digital service.

LESCO customers can open the official Installment Powers page under Customer Services. Its Whom to Contact page points customers to the relevant officer. Check current limits before applying. This is a LESCO example, not a national route.

Check the service description first. Does it accept installment requests? Or does it handle complaints and due-date questions only?

Use details printed on your bill or published on the DISCO's official website. Ignore numbers in unexpected messages.

After approval, our bank-app payment guide explains how to make a payment. It does not grant an installment plan.

Sample request

Sample request you can adapt

This sample is not an official form. Use it only after your DISCO confirms it accepts written requests.

  • To: Customer service or the authorized officer at [DISCO name].
  • Subject: Request to pay electricity dues in installments.
  • Name: [consumer name].
  • Reference number: [number from bill].
  • Bill month and amount: [details].
  • Request type: [current bill / old arrears].
  • Please review my request.
  • Confirm the decision and payment dates.
  • List any markup or LPS.
  • Name the computerized revised bill.

Keep the request factual. Never post a full CNIC number publicly. Do not send account details to an unverified contact.

Before agreeing

Questions to ask before accepting a plan

Before you pay electricity bill in installments Pakistan, confirm every term with your authorized DISCO.

  • Is this plan for the current bill, old arrears or both?
  • What is the first amount? What is its due date?
  • Must the first payment arrive by the printed due date?
  • Will later payments carry markup or LPS?
  • How many payments are approved? What are the dates?
  • Will the company issue a computerized revised bill?
  • What happens after a late installment or current bill?
  • Ask for the official payment channel. Ask how payments will appear on your account.

Write down the answers. Save the revised bill and approval message. Ask for clarity before making a partial payment.

If refused

If the request is refused or the terms are unclear

A DISCO may refuse a plan under its business requirements. Ask it to name the current local policy. Ask about other options.

An application does not protect you from LPS or disconnection by itself. Keep to the printed due date until the company confirms a change.

No clear answer? Contact the DISCO through its published customer service channel. Keep the request number. Our electricity-bill complaint guide covers the separate billing complaint route.

Billing complaint

If the bill amount is disputed

An installment plan will not correct a wrong reading, duplicate arrear or incorrect charge. File a billing complaint through the DISCO's official process.

Keep the bill and any earlier paid receipt. Save your complaint reference. Ask for the account ledger or a written explanation.

Clause 8.2.9 covers a NEPRA billing complaint or petition with a restraining order. It protects supply from disconnection. A complaint alone does not provide a restraining order. The clause also requires regular payment of current bills during the complaint.

For complaint steps, see our electricity-bill complaint guide.

Missed payment

If you miss an approved installment

Act quickly. Ask the DISCO for the amount needed to keep your account in good standing. Use its official channel.

Clause 8.2.6 protects supply during an approved installment plan. Keep every payment current. A further default leads to disconnection without another notice. Restoration follows payment of at least 50% of the dues.

Ask the DISCO how the clause applies to your schedule. Keep proof of every payment and message about a missed date.

A new request does not automatically cancel an earlier default. Get written confirmation from the company.

Account safety

Protect your connection and verify claims

Only the DISCO can approve its plan. A third party cannot promise approval or change the schedule on the DISCO's behalf. Do not send money to a personal account. Not for an application fee. Not to speed things up.

Download an app through your DISCO's official website or verified app-store listing. One company's app feature does not prove another company offers it.

Confirm the amount and schedule through an official channel before paying. Save the revised bill, receipt and request number.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay my current electricity bill in installments?

You can ask your DISCO. Clause 6.8.3 says companies may offer installments under their business requirements. Approval is not guaranteed.

Does NEPRA require every DISCO to approve installments?

No. The manual says DISCOs may offer the facility. Each company decides under its business requirements.

What markup applies to later installments?

For an approved current-month request, clause 6.8.2 sets 14% annual pro-rata markup on remaining installments. Check the revised bill for the amount.

Can I request installments for old arrears?

Ask your DISCO. Arrears may follow local rules. IESCO's FAQ gives one example, not a national policy.

Can I apply through an app or online?

Use a current official service only. The DISCO must list installment requests as a supported feature. Availability differs by company.

What happens if I miss an approved installment?

Clause 8.2.6 says a further default leads to disconnection without another notice. At least 50% of dues must be paid for restoration. Contact the DISCO quickly.

Final step

Final step: get the terms in writing

Ask your own DISCO whether your bill qualifies. Separate current charges from arrears and disputed amounts.
