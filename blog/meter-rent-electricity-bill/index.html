Bill par ek chhoti si line hoti hai jo har mahina waisi ki waisi rehti hai. Na units ke sath barhti hai, na FPA ki tarah ghat-ti hai. Meter rent.

Log isay ignore karte hain, kyun ke ye chhoti lagti hai. Lekin is line ke bare mein internet par jo likha hai, us mein se zyadatar ghalat hai.

Ek calculator kehta hai Rs 10 se Rs 25. Doosra kehta hai Rs 12 se Rs 50. Teesra kehta hai Rs 15 se Rs 40, aur meter ki umar par munhasir hai. Chaitha KE ke three phase par Rs 500 se Rs 800 batata hai.

Ye sab ek doosre se nahi milte. Aur is ka matlab hai ke in mein se zyadatar ghalat hain.

Sach ye hai ke meter rent ka rate poore Pakistan mein ek hi hai. IESCO ho ya K-Electric, sab ki tarif ek hi number kehti hai. Aur ek aur baat jo shayad aap ne kahin na parhi ho. February 2026 ke baad zyadatar gharon par ye charge lagta hi nahi.

Is guide mein meter rent ki poori wazahat hai. Sarkari rate, paanch official sources, aur woh wajah ke aap ke bill par ye line ab nazar kyun nahi aati.

Quick answer

Meter rent, four sentences mein

Meter rent DISCO ke meter ka mahana kiraya hai. Is ka sarkari naam minimum monthly customer charge hai, aur ye tab bhi lagta hai jab aap ne koi bijli istemal na ki ho.

Ghar ke single phase connection par Rs 75 mahana hai. Three phase par Rs 150 mahana. Ye poore Pakistan mein ek hi rate hai, K-Electric samait.

Lekin February 2026 se jin connections par naya per-kW fixed charge lagta hai, un par ye minimum charge lagti hi nahi. Is liye aaj kal zyadatar bill par meter rent ki line hoti hi nahi.

Agar aap ka bill Rs 75 se zyada dikhata hai, to woh meter rent nahi hai. Woh fixed charge hai, jo aap ke sanctioned load ke hisaab se lagta hai.

Definition

Meter rent hai kya cheez?

Meter aap ke ghar lagta hai, lekin woh aap ki milkiyat nahi hai. Woh DISCO ka hai. Meter rent us equipment ka mahana kiraya hai.

Lekin is ka sarkari naam thora alag hai. NEPRA ke tariff mein isay minimum monthly customer charge kehte hain. Ye naam zaroori hai, kyun ke is se ek baat saaf ho jati hai.

Ye charge is liye hai ke DISCO ko connection barqarar rakhne ka kharcha uthana parta hai. Meter, wiring, billing system aur line ki maintenance. Ye sab tab bhi chalti hai jab aap ne ek unit bhi istemal na ki ho.

Is liye ye charge zero units par bhi lagta hai. Agar aap poora mahina ghar se bahar rahein aur meter zero rahe, tab bhi ye line aayegi.

Ghaur ki jiye ke ye tax nahi hai. Yeh DISCO ki apni service ka kiraya hai. Tax woh hota hai jo sarkar ki aamdani ke liye lagaya jaye. Is farq ki tafseel hamari taxes wali guide mein hai.

Official rate

Sarkari rate kitna hai

Ye poore Pakistan mein ek hi hai. NEPRA ke tariff ke mutabiq:

Connection Ghar (Residential) Business (Commercial)
Single phaseRs 75 mahanaRs 175 mahana
Three phaseRs 150 mahanaRs 350 mahana

Zyadatar gharon ke liye jo number matter karta hai woh do hain. Single phase Rs 75, three phase Rs 150.

Ye rate units par munhasir nahi hai. 50 units istemal karein ya 500, ye wahi rahega. Aur ye har mahina waisa ka waisa rehta hai.

Naming

Isay "minimum charge" kyun kehte hain

Kyun ke ye ek floor hai, ek farsh. Is ka matlab hai ke aap ke bill ki kam se kam yahi raqam hogi, chahe aap ne kuch bhi istemal na kiya ho.

Sochiye aap ek mahina ghar se bahar thay. Units zero. Energy charge zero. FPA zero. Tab bhi bill zero nahi aayega. Rs 75 aayega.

Ye DISCO ke liye connection barqarar rakhne ka kharcha cover karta hai. Meter khara hai, line jari hai, billing system chal raha hai. Ye sab chalta rehta hai chahe aap bijli istemal karein ya na karein.

Is liye sarkari naam "minimum monthly customer charge" zyada mufeed hai. Meter rent kehne se lagta hai ke sirf meter ka kiraya hai, jab ke ye poori connection ki buniyadi qeemat hai.

Edge case

Zero units par bhi kyun lagta hai

Ye sawal aksar poochha jata hai, khas kar un logon ki taraf se jo ghar chhor kar jaate hain ya chhuttiyon par hote hain.

Jawab tariff ki language mein saaf hai. Official notification kehti hai ke ye charge lagay ga "even if no energy is consumed". Yani zero units hone par bhi.

Agar aap ne poora mahina ghar khali chhora tha aur phir bhi Rs 75 ka bill aaya, to woh ghalti nahi hai. Woh sahi hai.

Lekin agar aap ne connection hi permanently band kar diya hai, to ye charge nahi lagna chahiye. Us ke liye DISCO se formal disconnection karwana parta hai, sirf meter ghuma dena kafi nahi.

Sab se ahem baat

February 2026 se zyadatar log ye dete hi nahi

Ab woh baat jo shayad aap ne kahin na parhi ho.

February 2026 mein NEPRA ne tariff mein ek bari tabdeeli ki. S.R.O. 279(I)/2026 ke tehat ek naya fixed charge aaya, jo aap ke sanctioned load ke per kW ke hisaab se lagta hai.

Aur us notification mein ek note hai jo sab kuch badal deta hai. IESCO ki tarif ke alfaz mein:

Where monthly fixed charges are applicable, no minimum charges shall be applicable on such consumers, even if no energy consumed.

Is ka seedha matlab ye hai. Jin connections par naya per-kW fixed charge lagta hai, un par Rs 75 ya Rs 150 wali minimum charge lagti hi nahi. Donon ek sath nahi lagte.

Is liye aaj kal zyadatar gharon ke bill par meter rent ki line nazar hi nahi aati. Is ki jagah fixed charge ki line hoti hai, jo kaafi bari hoti hai.

Agar aap apne bill par meter rent dhoondh rahe hain aur nahi mil rahi, to is ka matlab ye nahi ke aap ko chhoot mil gayi hai. Is ka matlab hai ke aap par fixed charge lag raha hai.

Comparison

Meter rent vs fixed charge vs TV fee

Bill par chaar chhoti lines aapas mein mil jati hain. Inhein alag alag pehchanna zaroori hai.

Line Raqam Kis par munhasir
Fixed chargeRs 200 se Rs 675 per kWSanctioned load aur slab
Meter rentRs 75 ya Rs 150Single ya three phase
Minimum chargeRs 75 ya Rs 150Ek floor hai, izafi nahi

Ek line ka test. Agar woh raqam aap ke kW barhne ke sath barhti hai, to woh fixed charge hai. Baqi teeno flat hain.

Farq samajhne ka asan tareeqa ye hai. Meter rent aur minimum charge dono Rs 75 ya Rs 150 ke hain, kyun ke ye darasal ek hi cheez ke do naam hain. Fixed charge alag hai, aur aaj kal wohi bara hota hai.

Fixed charge ki mukammal tafseel, har slab ke rates aur worked examples ke sath, hamari fixed charges wali guide mein hain.

Worked maths

Ek misaal: Rs 75 sochna aur Rs 700 dena

Farz karein aap ka ghar 2 kW ke sanctioned load par hai aur aap 300 units istemal karte hain. Aap unprotected category mein hain.

Agar aap sirf meter rent ke hisaab se sochein, to aap ka khayal hoga ke fix raqam Rs 75 hai.

Lekin asal mein kya lagta hai:

Soch Raqam Hisab
Meter rent (jo log sochte hain)Rs 75Flat, single phase
Fixed charge (jo asal mein lagta hai)Rs 700Rs 350 per kW × 2 kW
Farq9.3 gunaRs 700 ÷ Rs 75

Yani aap ke bill ki fix raqam Rs 75 nahi, Rs 700 hai. Ye woh jagah hai jahan log sab se zyada chonkte hain.

Is liye agar aap ka bill Rs 75 se kahin zyada fix raqam dikhata hai, to ghabrayein nahi. Zyadatar sanadon par ab fixed charge lagta hai, meter rent nahi.

Karachi

K-Electric ke consumers ke liye

Karachi mein K-Electric ka apna tariff hai, aur us ke unit rates alag hain. Lekin minimum charge wale figures bilkul wahi hain.

KE ki apni official FAQ kehti hai ke zero istemal par single phase residential par Rs 75 aur three phase residential par Rs 150 lagta hai.

Commercial ke liye bhi wahi structure hai. Single phase Rs 175, three phase Rs 350. Ye ex-WAPDA DISCOs ke commercial rates se bilkul match karte hain.

Is liye meter rent ke matter par Karachi aur baqi Pakistan mein koi farq nahi. Ek hi number hai.

KE ke bill ki mukammal tafseel ke liye hamari K-Electric bill guide dekhein.

Business

Commercial connections par kitna lagta hai

Ghar ke rates to sab ko maloom hote hain, lekin business wale kam log mention karte hain. Wo bhi official tariff mein darj hain.

Wajah ye hai ke commercial connections ki maintenance aur billing ka kharcha zyada hota hai, aur ye tariff mein shamil hai.

Step by step

Apne bill par meter rent kaise check karein

  1. Apni taaza bill kholein, chahe kaghaz wali ho ya PITC se download ki hui duplicate bill.
  2. Energy charge ke neechay chhoti lines wala block dhoondhein.
  3. Wahan "Meter Rent", "M/Rent" ya "Minimum Charges" likha ho sakta hai. Har DISCO ka lafz thora alag hai.
  4. Agar Rs 75 ya Rs 150 nazar aaye, to woh meter rent hai. Aur aap par fixed charge nahi lag raha.
  5. Agar koi bari raqam hai jo aap ke kW se milti hai, to woh fixed charge hai, meter rent nahi.
  6. Agar koi bhi line nazar na aaye, to aap lifeline ya usi tarah ki category mein ho sakte hain.

Agar aap ko apni duplicate bill online chahiye, to online bill check wala tareeqa dekhein.

Reality check

Kya meter rent kam ho sakta hai?

Seedha jawab hai, nahi. Meter rent ek fixed tariff item hai. Is par aap ke units ka koi asar nahi, aur ye units kam karne se ghat-ti nahi.

Lekin do haqeeqi raaste hain.

  • Khud ka meter lagwayein. Agar meter aap ne kharida hai, to rent nahi lagna chahiye. Is ke liye DISCO se maloomat karwaein aur apna meter purchase record rakhein.
  • Ghalat charge par correction karwaein. Agar aap ka meter aap ki apni milkiyat hai aur phir bhi rent lag raha hai, to revenue office se correction karwa sakte hain.

Ek baat wazeh rahe. Jo log meter rent "bachane" ke liye units kam karte hain, woh ghalat fehmi mein hain. Units kam karne se energy charge ghat-ti hai, meter rent nahi.

Agar aap ka maqsad bill kam karna hai, to units aur sanctioned load par nazar rakhna zyada mufeed hai. Is ki tafseel hamari bill itna zyada kyun hai wali guide mein hai.

Sources

Sahi maloomat kahan se milti hai

Meter rent ke numbers par itna ikhtilaf is liye hai ke log doosron se naqal karte hain, sarkari sanad se nahi. Hamesha in zariyon se tasdeeq karein.

  • Apne DISCO ki tarif guide. Har DISCO NEPRA ki notification apni website par shaya karta hai. Wahan A-1 residential ka table dekhein.
  • NEPRA (nepra.org.pk). Har notification S.R.O. number ke sath yahan milti hai. Is waqt S.R.O. 279(I)/2026 nafiz hai.
  • Apni apni bill. Sab se aakhiri sanad. Jo figure bill par likha hai, wahi lagoo hai.

Ek nuance bhi samajh lein. Kuch DISCO ki websites purani notification dikhate hain. Minimum charge (Rs 75 / Rs 150) dono purani aur nayi notification mein same hai, lekin fixed charge ka tareeqa February 2026 mein badla tha. Is liye agar koi site fixed charge ko "Rs 200, Rs 400, Rs 600" jaisi flat raqam mein dikhaye, to woh purani hai.

NEPRA kaise kaam karta hai, yeh samajhne ke liye hamari NEPRA tariff guide dekhein.

Checklist

Meter rent samajhne ki aakhri checklist

  • Sarkari rate Rs 75 single phase, Rs 150 three phase hai. Ghar ke liye.
  • Ye poore Pakistan mein ek hai, K-Electric samait. DISCO-wise nahi badalta.
  • Is ka sarkari naam minimum monthly customer charge hai. Zero units par bhi lagta hai.
  • Zyadatar calculators jo Rs 10 se Rs 50 likhte hain, woh ghalat hain.
  • February 2026 se jin par fixed charge lagta hai, un par ye lagti hi nahi.
  • Agar aap ki fix line Rs 75 se zyada hai, to woh fixed charge hai, meter rent nahi.
  • Commercial par Rs 175 single phase, Rs 350 three phase hai.
  • Units kam karne se meter rent nahi ghat-ti. Sirf apna meter lagwa kar ya correction se.
FAQs

Aksar poochhe jane wale sawalat

Meter rent kitni hoti hai?

Ghar ke single phase connection par Rs 75 mahana aur three phase par Rs 150 mahana hai. Ye NEPRA ke tariff ke mutabiq hai aur poore Pakistan mein ek hi rate hai, K-Electric samait. Commercial connections ke liye single phase Rs 175 aur three phase Rs 350 hai.

Kya meter rent har DISCO mein alag hoti hai?

Nahi. IESCO, MEPCO, HAZECO aur K-Electric sab ki tarif Rs 75 aur Rs 150 hi kehti hai. Ye NEPRA ka ek hi qaumi tariff hai, is liye company ke hisaab se farq nahi parta. Jo farq nazar aata hai woh fixed charge ka hota hai, jo sanctioned load par munhasir hai.

Mere bill par meter rent ki line nazar hi nahi aati. Kya mujhe chhoot hai?

Ho sakta hai. February 2026 se jin connections par naya per-kW fixed charge lagta hai, un par minimum charge lagti hi nahi. NEPRA ke notification mein saaf likha hai ke jahan fixed charges nafiz hon, wahan minimum charges nafiz nahi hongi. Is liye agar aap ko Rs 75 ki line nazar nahi aati, to zyadatar imkaan ye hai ke aap par fixed charge lag raha hai.

Zero units istemal karne par bhi meter rent kyun lagti hai?

Kyun ke ye poori connection ki buniyadi qeemat hai, sirf meter ka kiraya nahi. DISCO ko meter, line aur billing system barqarar rakhna parta hai chahe aap bijli istemal karein ya na karein. NEPRA ka notification kehta hai ke ye charge lagay ga even if no energy is consumed. Agar aap ne connection permanently band kar diya hai, to alag baat hai.

Kya units kam karne se meter rent kam ho jayegi?

Nahi. Meter rent ek flat mahana charge hai jo units par munhasir nahi hai. Units kam karne se energy charge ghat-ti hai, meter rent nahi. Meter rent se bachne ka sirf ek tareeqa hai ke aap apna meter khud lagwa lein, ya agar ghalat charge ho raha ho to revenue office se correction karwaein.

Meter rent aur fixed charge mein kya farq hai?

Meter rent flat Rs 75 ya Rs 150 hai aur sirf single ya three phase par munhasir hai. Fixed charge Rs 200 se Rs 675 per kW hai aur aap ke sanctioned load aur consumption slab par munhasir hai. Feb 2026 se zyadatar gharon par fixed charge lagta hai aur meter rent nahi, kyun ke notification kehti hai ke donon ek sath nahi lagte.

Kya K-Electric ke consumers alag meter rent dete hain?

Nahi. KE ki apni official FAQ kehti hai ke residential single phase par Rs 75 aur three phase par Rs 150 lagta hai, bilkul ex-WAPDA DISCOs ki tarah. Commercial ke liye bhi wahi Rs 175 aur Rs 350 hain. Sirf unit rates aur slab structure alag hain, minimum charge wala number poore Pakistan mein ek hai.
