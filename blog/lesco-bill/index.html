Before you start

What you need to know before checking

Keep your 14-digit reference number or customer ID ready. It is printed on any older LESCO bill, usually near the top of the bill copy. If you are checking for a shop, tenant, or family member, use the exact number printed for that connection.

Also verify that you are checking the correct month and the correct meter. Many consumers accidentally use an old bill from another address, which brings up the wrong due amount.

Fastest route: copy the reference number exactly as shown on the bill, without spaces, then paste it into the bill checker.

Reading the bill

Understand the charges on your LESCO bill

Most confusion comes from a few line items: energy charges, fuel price adjustment, taxes, surcharges, arrears, and sometimes previous balance adjustments. The total due is not just “units × rate”; it is the full billed calculation for that month.

Common line items you'll see

  • Energy charges: calculated from your consumed units and the applicable tariff.
  • Fuel price adjustment: changes from month to month based on fuel cost.
  • Tax and duties: government-related charges applied on the bill.
  • Arrears or adjustments: old balances, corrections, or installment entries.
Pro tip: compare units more than totals. If units are much higher than your usual month, check whether the reading date, meter change, or seasonal usage explains the jump.
Payment

How to pay your LESCO bill online

You can pay through banking apps, mobile wallets, payment portals, or supported ATM/banking channels depending on your provider. Use only trusted channels and confirm the consumer number before you press pay.

  1. Choose your payment channelUse your bank app, mobile wallet, or the official payment route you already trust.
  2. Enter the reference numberPaste the same 14-digit number or customer ID requested by the payment screen.
  3. Verify bill month and amountMake sure the month and payable amount match the bill you checked.
  4. Save the receiptKeep a screenshot or transaction ID until the payment appears settled.
Good practice: pay a few days before the due date, especially around weekends or payment gateway maintenance windows.
Fix issues

What to do if your LESCO bill looks wrong

If the bill seems too high, do not ignore it. First compare the current meter reading with the reading printed on your bill. Then check whether the issue is usage, a meter problem, a tariff change, or a correction entry.

  • Compare the current meter reading with the billed reading.
  • Look for duplicate charges or unusual arrears.
  • Confirm the correct reference number and bill month.
  • Contact LESCO support or visit the local office if the meter reading is wrong.
Important: if you see a major mismatch between the physical meter and billed reading, report it quickly instead of waiting for the next bill cycle.
Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I check my LESCO bill without a reference number?

Usually no. The reference number or customer ID is the key identifier used by the billing system.

Can I download a duplicate bill?

Yes, after checking the bill online you can save, print, or download the duplicate copy if the portal provides it.

Does BijleeBill store my reference number?

No. We send you to the official channel. Your number is not stored on our servers.
