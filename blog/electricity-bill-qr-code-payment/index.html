Start here

That square code on a bill can lead to different services. First, find out what it is meant to open.

An electricity bill QR code payment works only with a provider-approved QR and a supported app.

K-Electric tells its customers to scan the QR on a KE bill through a banking app. Its official bill guide says customers can pay instantly.

That advice applies to KE customers. It doesn't cover every utility or app. For the manual route, see our safe bank-app payment guide.

Quick answer

Quick answer: can I pay by scanning the bill QR?

Electricity bill QR code payment is not available everywhere. K-Electric's official guide confirms a banking-app route for KE customers. Start with your own provider's instructions. A QR on a redesigned bill may open information or another digital service. Continue only with a code your utility names for payment. Then check the app details before approval.

QR types

What can a QR code on a bill do?

A QR code stores information. What happens next depends on the service it opens.

Code or scan result What it may do Safe interpretation
Payment QR Opens a payment flow inside a supported banking or payment app. Continue only under the utility's stated payment instructions.
Information or service QR Opens bill details or another digital service. It is not proof of payment. Check the utility's official instructions.
App-download QR Opens an app-store page or download information. It installs or points to an app. It does not settle a bill.
Bill barcode May fill a reference number in a bill-payment form. Scanning a reference is not the same as authorizing payment.
Possible benefit Limit to remember
A supported scan may fill bill details. Check each field before approval.
It may reduce manual entry. Support varies by utility and app.
It can start a payment flow quickly. A scan alone does not confirm payment.

A Ministry notice from June 2026 described redesigned-bill QRs as links to extra information and digital services. Read the announcement. It did not say every code starts a payment.

SBP's payment QR standard covers merchant and P2P payments. Raast P2M also supports merchant QR payments. See SBP's P2M overview. That does not show whether a particular utility bill or app supports scan-to-pay.

Provider support

Which providers support electricity bill QR code payment?

K-Electric is the one direct official example we found for bill-QR payment. Its bill guide says customers can scan its QR through a banking app and pay instantly.

The Ministry's June 2026 release discussed redesigned bills for DISCO customers. It described the QR codes as access points for supplementary information and digital services. It did not confirm payment functionality for those codes.

The release gave rollout details for June 2026. Those details are not a current rollout list.

For LESCO, IESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO or PESCO, check the latest official payment instructions. Do the same elsewhere. The QR's presence does not confirm support. SBP's QR standard does not confirm it either.

It may save typing. Still, the code's purpose may not be clear. Check its label and payment details before approving a debit.

Before scanning

What to check before scanning

Keep your latest bill nearby. Check the company name and the code's label.

Look for a payment instruction on the bill or utility website. No instruction? Pause.

Use the official banking or payment app installed on your phone. Open it yourself. Do not start from a payment link in an unexpected message.

Check the printed code first. Avoid one covered by a sticker, damaged or altered.

A hard-to-read code? Use an official alternative. Contact your provider through a number or website you already trust.

In your app

Open the app's QR payment feature

App menus differ by bank and payment service. Some apps offer QR payment or scan. Use this option.

Some apps may show a payment prompt. Other apps may lack support for the utility's bill QR.

Do not copy menu steps from another bank's guide. Follow the instructions inside your own app.

App rejects the code? Stop there. Check the utility's official instructions for another supported route. Get provider confirmation first.

Payment steps

How to complete electricity bill QR code payment

Use these steps only for a QR your provider identifies for payment.

  1. Confirm the code's purpose. Check the bill label or the utility's official payment page.
  2. Open your official app. Sign in as usual. Choose its QR payment or scan option.
  3. Scan the bill code. Keep the whole code inside the app's scan frame.
  4. Review the payment screen. Check the biller, reference, period and amount shown in the app.
  5. Authorize only after a match. Follow the app's own security prompt.
  6. Save the result. Keep the receipt or transaction reference until the payment status is clear.

The exact screens vary. Some apps fill fields from the code. Other apps request more details.

Never guess at a missing field. Ask your bank or utility through an official support channel.

Before approval

Verify the biller, reference, and amount

Take a moment on the confirmation screen. Read each detail before you tap the final button.

Check Compare with Stop when
Biller or payee Your utility's official payment instructions. The name is unfamiliar or conflicts with those instructions.
Reference or account The current bill you intend to pay. The number belongs to another connection or cannot be checked.
Billing period The month or due details on your latest bill. The app shows a different period you cannot explain.
Amount The bill and any applicable late amount. The number differs and the app gives no clear reason.
Funding account and fee The account you selected and any fee shown in the app. You did not choose that account or do not accept the displayed fee.

Some apps leave out fields. That does not count as a match.

A different payee or amount means stop. Check the bill again, then contact the bank or utility before authorizing payment.

If a webpage opens

What if the QR opens a webpage?

A webpage does not mean your bill is paid. It may show information or open another digital service.

Check the page against your utility's official instructions. Do not enter a bank password, card PIN or OTP on an unclear page.

Some official services ask for bill details. Keep banking credentials inside the official app. Open a webpage only through a direct link from the utility.

No payment confirmation in your bank app? Do not treat the scan alone as a receipt.

Troubleshooting

What if your app cannot scan the QR?

Your app may not support the code or provider. The print on the bill may also be damaged or hard to read.

Do not select a different company to get past an error. Do not keep scanning a code with an unclear purpose.

Use the provider's listed alternative. A bank app may offer a manual biller search and an identifier field.

The app may ask for a reference number or another bill identifier. Use the field label as your guide.

KE's bill guide calls the Account Number its most important customer reference. Use it only in a field labelled Account Number.

See our safe bank-app payment guide for the manual biller route. For other options, read how to pay an electricity bill online.

Payment status

How to confirm the payment result

Wait for the app to show a final result. Save the receipt or transaction reference.

For a closed screen or unclear status, check transaction history. Note the date and amount.

A bank confirmation shows the app's result. Your utility's bill page may take longer to show a new balance.

For a pending or unclear debit, contact the bank through its official channel. Ask it to check the transaction reference.

Do not pay again until the first transaction's status is clear. Keep the receipt for any later question.

Fees and timing

Are QR payments always instant or free?

One rule does not apply to every utility or app. K-Electric's bill guide says customers can pay its QR instantly through a banking app.

That statement does not promise instant payment or bill posting for every provider. Check the result inside your own app.

Review any fee shown before approval. Ask your bank about a charge you do not understand.

SBP's payment QR and Raast P2M rules describe payment infrastructure. They do not confirm each biller's channel, fee or update time.

Account safety

Protect your account during a QR payment

Open the app yourself. Keep your phone and banking app updated.

Do not follow unexpected bill-payment links. A genuine-looking code can still lead to the wrong place.

Keep app passwords, card PINs and OTPs to yourself. Do not share them with callers or helpers.

SBP says financial institutions must not require customers to provide OTPs verbally to their officers. This includes call-centre agents. Read the SBP consumer conduct framework.

An altered code? Stop. Use the utility's official payment page or contact centre.

Know the difference

A barcode scan is not a QR payment

Some bill-payment apps scan a barcode to fill a reference number. The user still chooses a biller and authorizes the payment.

Our JazzCash bill-payment guide shows a barcode scanner beside the reference field. It fills in the number. That does not pay the bill.

Keep the code's purpose clear. Payment QRs, information QRs and reference barcodes can look similar on paper.

Troubleshooting

Common QR-scan problems

The code is blurred or damaged

Try a clear, current bill. Check the provider's official page for the correct route.

No scan? Use the manual biller method. Do not try to guess the number.

The app rejects the code

The app may not support this provider's QR payment. Check the utility's instructions first.

Use another official payment route only after confirming the correct biller.

The payee does not look familiar

Stop before authorization. Compare the app's name with the provider's official instructions.

Ask your bank or utility for help. Do not send money to a name you cannot verify.

The amount does not match

Check the latest bill and the due date. The amount due may change after the due date. Check your utility's current payment instructions.

Stop before approval. Compare the total against your latest bill. Ask the provider or bank to explain any difference.

Choose the route

A simple decision guide

  • Provider confirms the payment purpose: Match the app details. Review the funding account and authorize inside the app.
  • The code opens an information page or an unclear destination: stop and check the utility's official instructions.
  • The app cannot scan or identify the bill: use the provider's official manual biller route.
  • The debit or result is unclear: save the reference and contact the bank before retrying.

A scan is only the start. Check the details, then confirm the final status.

Final check

Before you tap confirm

  • The utility identifies this QR as a payment code.
  • The biller matches the provider on my latest bill.
  • The bill or account reference belongs to my connection.
  • Compare any billing period with the latest bill.
  • The amount is clear and matches the bill or due-date total.
  • I chose the right funding account and understand any displayed fee.
  • I am authorizing inside the official app.
  • I saved the receipt or transaction reference.

Missing detail? Stop and check first.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does every electricity bill in Pakistan have a payment QR code?

No. Check the instructions from your specific utility. A QR's presence alone does not prove payment support.

Can I pay by scanning the code on my KE bill?

K-Electric's guide says customers can scan the QR on a KE bill through a banking app and pay instantly. Follow KE's current instructions.

Can I make electricity bill QR code payment with any app?

Not always. App support varies by provider and biller. Check both sources before continuing.

What if the QR opens a webpage?

A webpage may show information or a digital service. It is not proof of payment. Use only pages named in official provider instructions.

What if my account was debited but the bill still looks unpaid?

Save the transaction reference. Check your bank history and ask the bank to trace the payment. Do not pay again until its status is clear.

What if my app cannot scan the QR?

Use the utility's official alternative, such as a manual biller route in your bank app. Check the requested identifier carefully.
