Karachi reads a different bill. K-Electric runs its own billing system instead of the PITC portal. It has its own tariff names and its own page. And one key: the 13-digit account number.

This pillar walks the KE bill end to end. Where the number sits. What the blocks mean. How to open the official copy on ke.com.pk. And how to pay without a paper bill.

In a hurry? The checker routes K-Electric straight to the official page.

Quick answer

How to check a K-Electric bill online

Open the checker, choose K-Electric, and enter the 13-digit account number from any old bill. The official KE page shows the current amount, due date and history. Save the PDF; pay through any bank app or wallet with the same number.

No account, no fee, no app install. The page works on any phone browser.

Anatomy

What a KE bill actually shows

The account number block

The 13-digit account number sits near your name and address and appears on every receipt and SMS alert. It is the only key the official page asks for.

Tariff and peak blocks

KE bills split usage into peak and off-peak for time-of-use tariffs, and name the tariff class. Domestic, commercial or industrial. The slab rates differ from WAPDA's layout, but the logic is the same: units times slab rates plus taxes.

Due date and surcharge

The payable amount and due date sit together. Once the date passes, the next bill carries the late surcharge. Avoidable with one reminder.

Step by step

Check your KE bill in five steps

  1. Open the K-Electric route on the checker. It hands you ke.com.pk in a new tab. Verify the address bar before typing.
  2. Type the 13-digit account number. Copy it from any old bill or a wallet receipt. Digits only.
  3. Confirm name and address. One wrong digit opens nothing; the name check protects against near-misses.
  4. Read the amount, due date and history. Peak/off-peak splits appear in the usage block.
  5. Save the PDF, then pay. The same account number pays through banks, JazzCash and Easypaisa.
Numbers

KE account number vs WAPDA reference

Thirteen, not fourteen

KE's account number is 13 digits. WAPDA DISCOs use the 14-digit reference plus a 10-digit consumer number. The formats never mix; a KE lookup wants exactly thirteen digits.

Where to find it without a bill

Wallet receipts, bank history and KE SMS alerts all carry the account number. The number-finding guide shows the exact spot on paper.

Lost it entirely?

The KE helpline recovers the number for the account holder with the CNIC. One call ends the search.

Troubleshooting

KE bill not loading? The usual fixes

Most failures are a mistyped digit or a bill not yet posted for the month. Feeder-level delays around monsoon can postpone posting by a few days. If the same number fails on two devices over two days, use the complaint form on ke.com.pk. Keep the tracking number. The bill-not-received guide covers the paper side.

Payment

Paying a KE bill

Banking apps, bank counters, JazzCash and Easypaisa all take the 13-digit account number and show the amount before you confirm. The paid status can take a day or two to update; the receipt covers you until then. The KE duplicate bill guide covers printing and filing the copy.

Warnings

Safety warnings

Use only ke.com.pk for the bill view. KE never asks for a card number, OTP or wallet PIN on the bill page. Lookalike domains spell K-Electric slightly differently to fool a quick search. Check the address bar every time.

Checklist

The one-minute checklist

  • 13-digit account number counted
  • Name and address verified on screen
  • Amount and due date noted
  • PDF saved with the month in the file name
  • Payment made before the due date
  • Receipt filed beside the PDF
FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How many digits is a K-Electric account number?

Thirteen. KE does not use the WAPDA 14-digit reference or the 10-digit consumer number.

Can I check a KE bill on bill.pitc.com.pk?

No. KE runs its own system; the official bill view lives on ke.com.pk, which the checker opens directly.

Is the KE online bill valid for payment?

Yes. The online copy mirrors the paper bill, and every payment channel accepts the account number.

Can I check a KE bill by CNIC or name?

No. The public page accepts only the 13-digit account number; CNIC-based recovery happens through the KE helpline for the account holder.

I paid but KE still shows unpaid. Why?

Status updates take 24 to 48 hours. Keep the receipt and recheck the next day.

What if my paper bill never arrives?

The online copy is the working bill. The bill-not-received guide walks the fixes, and the duplicate guide covers printing a full-page copy.

Does KE have peak and off-peak rates?

Time-of-use tariffs split usage into peak and off-peak blocks on the bill. The slab lines show both.
