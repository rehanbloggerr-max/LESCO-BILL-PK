Searching for “electricity bill late payment surcharge Pakistan”? Start with the due date on your bill.

Pay the full amount by that date. That is the surest way to avoid LPS.

K-Electric’s Know Your Bill FAQ lists a 5% charge for the next three days. It lists 10% from day four onward.

Taxes and government duties are excluded from the billed amount used for LPS. Check your own bill before paying.

Start here

What is late payment surcharge?

Late payment surcharge is an extra charge on an unpaid electricity bill. Utilities shorten the name to LPS.

Some tariff documents call it a late payment charge.

The bill usually shows an amount payable within the due date. It may also show a higher amount after the due date.

Compare those figures before paying. Check the billed amount, due date, and any adjustment shown on the bill.

LPS is separate from fuel adjustments, taxes, and other regular bill entries. The applicable surcharge rate and its calculation base come from official tariff directions.

Do not assume the full bill total forms the calculation base.

Current rates

Electricity bill late payment surcharge Pakistan: rates at a glance

K-Electric’s Know Your Bill FAQ describes a two-step rate structure. It lists 5% for the first three days after the due date.

The rate rises to 10% on day four. NEPRA’s 2025 PESCO tariff conditions use the same rates.

K-Electric says the structure took effect on October 1, 2024.

When full payment is made LPS rate stated by K-Electric Calculation base
On or before the due date No LPS Not applicable
Within three days after the due date 5% Billed amount, excluding government taxes and duties
Four or more days after the due date 10% Billed amount, excluding government taxes and duties

The 2025 PESCO tariff conditions say 5% “may be levied” during the next three days. The conditions allow 10% afterward.

Check the due-date amounts printed on your bill. Ask your distribution company to explain any mismatch between the amount and payment date.

Those three days are not free. A 5% LPS can apply.

Pay by the due date to avoid it.

Read your bill

Read the two payable amounts on your bill

Many bills show two totals. One applies before the due date.

The other applies afterward. Compare the labels.

The difference can help you spot the LPS entry.

K-Electric’s updated bill separates current dues from outstanding dues.

Its Know Your Bill page calls the late amount “Amount Payable.”

Other DISCOs may use different labels.

The difference alone does not prove the charge is right. Arrears or adjustments may also affect the total.

Check current charges and previous balances separately.

Use this quick review before paying:

  1. Match the reference number to your connection.
  2. Confirm the bill month and printed due date.
  3. Find the amount payable within the due date.
  4. Find the amount payable after the due date.
  5. Compare the difference with the LPS line, if shown.
  6. Check whether taxes or duties appear in the calculation base.
  7. Save a copy of the bill before making payment.

Some bills may display only one payment figure or a different layout. Use the labels on your own bill.

Ask your DISCO to explain an unclear calculation.

Calculation

How to calculate the surcharge

Official tariff wording excludes government taxes and duties from the billed amount used for LPS. Use this base in your calculation.

Do not add excluded taxes or duties to it.

Basic calculation:

LPS = eligible billed amount × applicable percentage

Example: the eligible billed amount is Rs 10,000 before government taxes and duties. A 5% LPS adds Rs 500.

A 10% LPS adds Rs 1,000.

This is only an example. Your bill may include several line items.

The tariff wording does not define each item in this example. Ask the DISCO to explain a figure you cannot match.

Check these items:

  • The printed due date.
  • The date and time your payment was accepted.
  • The amount billed, excluding government taxes and duties.
  • The LPS rate applied.
  • Any “payable after due date” amount shown on the bill.
  • The receipt or transaction reference for your payment.
Payment timing

When can LPS apply?

LPS can apply after a bill’s due date. Full payment is required.

K-Electric lists 5% for the next three days. The rate becomes 10% on day four.

The printed due date matters. A request for more time does not itself change that date.

Follow the printed date. Change it only after your DISCO confirms an extension or issues a revised bill.

NEPRA’s Consumer Service Manual says an approved extension carries no LPS. Keep the approval message or revised bill.

That record confirms the new date.

Payment method matters too. The manual advises early deposits for cheques, drafts, and pay orders.

Submit them at least three days before the due date. Keep the deposit receipt.

LPS starts after the seventh day from delivery.

Extensions and installments

Due-date extensions and installments

NEPRA’s revised Consumer Service Manual sets out separate provisions for extensions and installments.

Situation What the manual says What to confirm
Approved due-date extension No LPS during the extended period The approved date and revised bill
Approved current-bill plan First on-time installment has no LPS or markup Approval and first payment date
Later installments 14% annual markup on a pro-rata basis Schedule and calculation shown by the DISCO

DISCOs may offer extensions or installments under their business requirements. Approval is not automatic.

Ask your company about its current process.

The manual requires a computerized bill after an installment plan is approved. Check it before paying.

Confirm the account, schedule, dates, amount, and markup.

Do not treat an installment request as an extension. Old arrears may follow different terms.

Ask the DISCO to confirm the arrangement in writing or through its official system.

Billing dispute

How to question an incorrect LPS charge

Check the basic details first. A date or posting mismatch can make the LPS look wrong.

  1. Compare the bill dates. Note the bill issue date, due date, and any revised date.
  2. Find your payment proof. Keep the receipt, bank reference, or successful transaction record.
  3. Check the calculation base. Compare the surcharge with the billed amount excluding government taxes and duties.
  4. Record late delivery if relevant. Keep evidence of the actual delivery date.
  5. Contact the DISCO. Use its official customer-service route and explain the specific mismatch.
  6. Keep the response. Save the complaint number, message, or written decision.

Use a short request: “Please review the LPS on bill [month], reference [number]. I paid on [date/time].

I received the bill on [date]. Please explain the rate and calculation.

Correct any error.”

This sample is not an official form. Follow your DISCO’s complaint instructions.

Do not send account details to an unverified number or social-media account.

A response may not solve the issue. Ask about the official escalation route.

Check NEPRA’s current complaint information. Keep the bill, payment proof, delivery evidence, and complaint number.

Pay by the printed date to avoid LPS.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the late payment surcharge on an electricity bill in Pakistan?

LPS is an added charge on a bill paid after its due date. K-Electric lists 5% for payment within three days after the due date.

It lists 10% from day four onward. The rate applies to the billed amount, excluding government taxes and duties.

Is the surcharge charged on government taxes and duties?

K-Electric and NEPRA exclude government taxes and duties from the billed amount used for LPS. Check the breakdown.

Ask your DISCO to explain an unclear figure.

How much LPS applies if I pay one day late?

The official rate schedule states 5% for payment within the next three days after the due date. It is not a fee-free period.

The billed amount excludes government taxes and duties from this calculation base.

What if my electricity bill arrives after the due date?

NEPRA tariff conditions cover bills served less than seven days before the due date. LPS starts after the seventh day from delivery.

Record the delivery date. Ask your DISCO to review the charge.

Can my DISCO extend the due date?

The Consumer Service Manual says LPS will not be imposed where the due date is extended. DISCOs may offer extensions according to their business requirements.

Wait for official confirmation before relying on a new date.

Can I pay my current bill in installments?

You can ask your DISCO. The manual says DISCOs may offer installments according to business requirements.

For an approved current-bill plan, the first installment has no markup or LPS. Pay it by the due date.

Later installments carry 14% annual pro-rata markup.
