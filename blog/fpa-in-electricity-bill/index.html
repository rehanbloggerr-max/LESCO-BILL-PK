Aksar is ka sabab woh line hai jis par log sab se kam ghaur karte hain. FPA. Fuel Price Adjustment.

Yeh ek aisi line hai jo har mahina badalti hai, aap ke kuch kiye baghair. Kabhi bill barh jata hai. Kabhi kam ho jata hai.

Masla yeh hai ke online is ke bare mein aadhi maloomat ghalat hai. Kuch sites har DISCO ke liye alag FPA rate chap deti hain. Kuch FPA aur FCA ko do alag charges batate hain. Kuch kehte hain ke yeh tax hai.

Yeh teeno baatein sahi nahi hain. Aur isi ghalatfehmi ki wajah se log apne bill ka sahi hisaab nahi laga pate.

Is guide mein FPA ki poori wazahat hai. Waziratan-e-bijli (Ministry of Energy) ki apni tareef se le kar NEPRA ke notification tak. Sath hi woh cheez bhi jo shayad aap ne kahin nahi parhi. Prepaid consumers ka FPA zero hota hai.

Quick answer

FPA in an electricity bill, in four sentences

FPA ek adjustment hai jo benzine ki tarah kaam karta hai. Bijli gharon ko chalane wala fuel har mahina mehnga ya sasta hota hai. Us farq ko aap ke bill mein FPA ke zariye adjust kiya jata hai.

Yeh koi tax nahi hai. Yeh ek hi qaumi rate hai, har DISCO ke liye alag nahi. NEPRA har mahina ek figure notify karta hai aur wohi figure LESCO se le kar K-Electric tak sab par lagta hai.

FPA aap ke units par lagta hai. Rate × units = FPA ki raqam. Is par GST bhi lagta hai, is liye asar thora barh kar nazar aata hai.

Agar aap lifeline, EV charging station ya prepaid consumer hain, to aap ka FPA zero hai. Is ki tafseel neeche section 11 mein hai.

Definition

FPA hai kya cheez?

Sab se pehle sarkari tareef dekh lete hain. Ministry of Energy apni website par FPA ko is tarah bayan karta hai.

Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA) is the difference between actual fuel charges component for a month and reference fuel charges component. In case of positive variation, the fuel price is charged to the consumer through electricity bill and for negative variation the consumer is given benefit of fuel price adjustment as per NEPRA Notification.

Is ka matlab simple hai. Har saal jo tariff NEPRA notify karta hai, us mein fuel ka ek andaza laga hota hai. Ke bijli banane mein Rs 7 ka fuel lagay ga. Isay kehte hain reference fuel charges.

Lekin asal mein fuel kitna pada? Woh to mahine khatam hone par pata chalta hai. Agar asal fuel cost Rs 8.20 aayi, to reference se Rs 1.20 zyada ho gaya.

Yeh Rs 1.20 ka farq hi FPA hai. Consumer se wasool kiya jata hai. Aur agar asal cost reference se kam ho, to yeh farq negative ho jata hai aur consumer ko faida milta hai.

Ghaur ki jiye ke FPA aur FCA do alag cheezein nahi hain. Fuel Price Adjustment aur Fuel Charges Adjustment ek hi charge ke do naam hain. NEPRA ke notifications mein aksar FCA likha hota hai. Purane bills aur DISCO ke software mein aksar FPA chapta hai. Dono ka matlab ek hai.

Logic

FPA ka wajood hi kyun hai?

Bijli ke ghar (power plants) coal, gas, RLNG ya furnace oil se chaltay hain. In ka fuel market rate par milta hai. Woh har mahina badalta hai. Kabhi RLNG mehnga ho jata hai. Kabhi barish se hydro sasta ho jata hai.

NEPRA saal mein ek baar tariff notify karta hai. Us waqt woh andaza lagata hai ke fuel ka kitna kharcha aayega. Lekin poore saal ke liye ek hi andaza theek nahi reh sakta.

Is liye ek structure banaya gaya hai. Reference cost tariff mein pehle se shamil hoti hai. Phir har mahina asal cost ka hisaab hota hai aur farq adjust kiya jata hai.

Is tarah do faiday hote hain. Consumer ko wohi fuel cost chukani parti hai jo asal mein lagi. Aur DISCO ko bhi apna kharcha recover karne ka raasta milta hai.

Yeh system sirf Pakistan ka nahi hai. Dunya bhar mein fuel adjustment clauses aam hain. Farq sirf itna hai ke kahin yeh saalana hota hai, kahin mahana.

Process

NEPRA yeh rate kaise tay karta hai?

Har mahina ek chalta hua cycle hai. Isay samajh lena zaroori hai, kyun ke isi se woh do mahine ki delay bhi samajh aati hai.

  1. Mahina khatam hota hai. CPPA-G (Central Power Purchasing Agency) us mahine ke fuel ka asal kharcha jama karta hai.
  2. CPPA-G NEPRA mein petition daalta hai. Ke actual cost itni thi, reference itni thi, farq itna hai. Is farq ko recover karne ki ijazat di jaye.
  3. NEPRA public hearing karti hai. Yeh hearing aam taur par agle mahine ke aakhir mein hoti hai.
  4. NEPRA data ko check karta hai. Aksar woh CPPA-G ke kuch dawe katte hain.
  5. Phir NEPRA apna decision notify karta hai. Ek S.R.O. number ke sath.
  6. DISCOs aur K-Electric ko hukum hota hai ke isay agle billing cycle mein alag line ke tor par dikhayein.

Yahan ek misaal dekh lete hain. August 2026 ke fuel ke liye CPPA-G ne Rs 1.7267 per unit maanga tha. NEPRA ne data check kiya to plant operators ke Rs 17,279 million ke dawe katt gay, jin mein se Rs 15,736 million sirf RLNG plants ke thay.

Nateeja yeh nikla ke asal fuel cost Rs 8.8265 ke bajaye Rs 8.2084 mani gayi. Aur FPA Rs 1.7267 ke bajaye Rs 1.1086 approve hua.

Yani hearing mein jana bekaar nahi jata. Is ek decision se qaumi sathah par lag bhag Rs 16 billion ka farq para.

Timing

Woh do mahine ki delay

Sab se aam sawaal yeh hai. August ka fuel, October ke bill par kyun?

Is ki wajah upar wala cycle hai. Fuel to mahine mein jalta hai. Lekin us ka hisaab lagane, hearing karne aur notification jari karne mein waqt lagta hai.

August 2026 ke liye yeh silsila kuch yun chala.

Is liye aap ke bill ki FPA line hamesha us fuel ki hoti hai jo lag bhag do mahine pehle jala tha. Yeh koi ghalti nahi hai. Yeh system ka hissa hai.

Ek aur nuance bhi hai. Agar koi bill notification se pehle chhap gaya ho, to NEPRA kehti hai ke adjustment agle mahine lagaya ja sakta hai. Is liye kabhi kabhi aap ko October ki bajaye November ke bill mein October wala FPA nazar aata hai.

September 2026 ke fuel ke liye CPPA-G ne Rs 2.56 per unit ki darkhwast ki hai. Is par hearing 29 October 2026 ko honi hai. Abhi yeh manzoorshuda nahi. Jo kuch bhi final ho ga, woh naye S.R.O. ke sath aayega.

Formula

Apna FPA khud kaise calculate karein

Formula itna simple hai ke aap isay mobile ke calculator par kar sakte hain.

FPA ki raqam = FPA rate × aap ke us mahine ke units

Bas do cheezein chahiye. Us mahine ka FPA rate, jo NEPRA notify karta hai. Aur aap ke bill par likhe units.

Ek misaal lete hain. Farz karein aap ke bill par 300 units hain aur us mahine ka FPA Rs 1.1086 hai.

300 × 1.1086 = Rs 332.58

Yeh raqam aap ke bill par FPA ki line mein likhi honi chahiye. Ab alag alag istemal ke liye dekh lete hain.

Comparison

FPA vs FCA vs QTA vs FC surcharge

Bill par ek se zyada adjustment lines hoti hain. Log inhein aapas mein mix kar dete hain. Yeh chaar alag cheezein hain.

Adjustment Kya hai Kitni baar Is waqt

FCAFPA hi hai, bas doosra naamHar mahinaRs 1.1086/unit
QTACapacity payments, transmission, O&MHar 3 mahineRs 0.5194/unit
FC surchargeFinancing cost surchargeFixed, tariff ka hissaRs 0.43/unit

Sab se ahem baat. FPA aur FCA ek hi cheez hain. NEPRA ke notifications mein Fuel Charges Adjustment likha hota hai. Aap ke bill par aksar FPA chapta hai. Do alag charges ka sochna ghalat hai.

QTA bilkul alag hai. Yeh quarterly tariff adjustment hai, jis mein fuel nahi balkay capacity payments, variable O&M, transmission (UoSC), market operator fee aur T&D losses par FCA ka asar shamil hota hai. Yeh har 3 mahine baad aata hai aur 3 mahino ke bills mein banta hua lagta hai.

FC surcharge aam taur par Rs 0.43 per unit hoti hai, lekin kai bills par Rs 3.23 per unit printed nazar aati hai. Farq ki wajah alag alag tareeqon se hisaab lagana hai. Is ki tafseel hamari taxes wali guide mein hai.

Credit

Negative FPA, jab bill kam ho jaye

FPA hamesha barhta nahi hai. Jab asal fuel cost reference se kam ho, to FPA negative ho jata hai. Aur tab yeh charge nahi balkay credit ban jata hai.

2026 mein yeh do baar hua. January ke bills par Rs 0.93 per unit ka negative adjustment laga. March ke bills par Rs 0.01 per unit ka.

March wala Rs 0.001 dekhne mein chhota hai, lekin isay ghaur se dekhein. Is ka matlab hai ke us mahine fuel ka asal kharcha aur tariff mein laga hua andaza lag bhag barabar thay. System theek kaam kar raha tha.

Bill par negative FPA aam taur par bracket mein ya minus ke sath chapta hai. Jaise (Rs 93) ya -Rs 93. Ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi. Yeh raqam aap ke bill se kat-ti hai, jarti nahi.

Exemptions

Kaun FPA se mustasna hai

Har NEPRA notification mein ek exemption wali clause hoti hai. 2026 ke notifications mein yeh teen group mustasna hain.

  • Lifeline consumers. Woh gharane jo bohot kam bijli istemal karte hain. Un par FPA lagta hi nahi.
  • Electric vehicle charging stations. EV charging ke liye bijli istemal karne wale connections par bhi chhoot hai.
  • Prepaid consumers. Jinho ne prepaid tariff ka intekhab kiya hai. Un ka FPA zero hai.

Wazeh rahe ke yeh chhoot har notification mein nazar aati hai. NEPRA har dafa in teeno groups ka naam le kar likhta hai ke FPA un par nafiz nahi hoga.

Lekin ek group par FPA lagta hai, jis ke bare mein aksar ghalat fehmi hoti hai. Incremental Consumption Package ke tehat istemal kiye gaye units. Notification mein saaf likha hota hai ke adjustment "incremental consumption package" ke units par bhi nafiz hoga.

Karachi

K-Electric ke consumers ke liye

Karachi mein K-Electric apna alag system chalata hai. Lekin FPA ke liye koi alag rate nahi hai.

Har NEPRA notification mein saaf likha hota hai ke yeh adjustment ex-WAPDA DISCOs aur K-Electric dono ke tamam consumers par nafiz hoga. Wahi rate, wahi mahina, wahi exemptions.

Is liye October 2026 ke bill par K-Electric ke consumer ka FPA bhi Rs 1.1086 per unit hai. Bil-kul LESCO, IESCO ya MEPCO ki tarah.

Farq sirf utna hai jo K-Electric ka apna tariff structure paida karta hai. Base rate alag ho sakta hai. Lekin FPA ki line ka rate qaumi hai.

K-Electric ke bill ki tafseel ke liye hamari K-Electric bill guide dekhein.

Comparison

Aap ka FPA parosi se alag kyun hai

Yeh aam ghalatfehmi hai ke har DISCO ka apna FPA rate hota hai. Kuch websites to DISCO ke hisaab se alag alag ranges hi chhap deti hain. LESCO ke liye 2.8 se 4.2, K-Electric ke liye 3.5 se 5.0.

Yeh theek nahi hai. FPA ek hi qaumi figure hai. Ek hi mahine ke liye, ek hi S.R.O. ke tehat, sab par lagta hai.

To phir aap ka FPA aap ke parosi se alag kyun nazar aata hai? Is ki chaar wajoohat hain.

Clarity

FPA koi tax nahi hai

Bill par FPA ko aksar taxes wale block mein dikhaya jata hai. Is liye log isay tax samajh baithte hain.

Yeh tax nahi hai. Tax woh hota hai jo sarkar ki aam aamdani ke liye lagaya jaye. Jaise GST, electricity duty ya income tax. In ki tafseel hamari taxes wali guide mein hai.

FPA cost pass-through hai. Sarkar koi paisa nahi leti. Yeh woh farq hai jo bijli banane walon ko asal mein fuel ka pada. Woh farq consumer se recover kiya jata hai, ya negative hone par consumer ko wapas diya jata hai.

Is farq ka amali faida yeh hai. Tax ko kam nahi kiya ja sakta. Lekin FPA par asar dala ja sakta hai, kyun ke yeh units ke hisaab se lagta hai. Jitni kam bijli, utna kam FPA.

Step by step

Apne bill par FPA kaise check karein

  1. Apni taaza bill kholein. Chahe kaghaz wali ho ya PITC se download ki hui duplicate bill.
  2. Energy charge wali line ke neechay dekhein. Wahan FPA ya FCA likha hota hai.
  3. Jo figure sath likha hai, woh do cheezein batata hai. Rate (per unit) aur total raqam.
  4. Apne units se rate ko multiply kar ke check karein. Barabar aana chahiye.
  5. Agar FPA ki line hai hi nahi, to aap lifeline ya prepaid ho sakte hain.
  6. Agar raqam negative hai, to bracket mein hogi. Woh aap ka credit hai.

Agar aap ko apni duplicate bill online chahiye, to online bill check wala tareeqa dekhein.

Myth-busting

FPA ke bare mein paanch aam ghalatfehmiyan

Ghalatfehmi 1. Har DISCO ka apna FPA rate hota hai

Nahi hota. NEPRA ek hi qaumi figure notify karta hai. LESCO ho ya K-Electric, rate ek hai. Jo farq nazar aata hai woh units ka hota hai, rate ka nahi.

Ghalatfehmi 2. FPA aur FCA do alag charges hain

Nahi. Yeh ek hi adjustment ke do naam hain. NEPRA ke S.R.O. mein aksar Fuel Charges Adjustment likha hota hai. Bills par aksar FPA chapta hai. Dono ek hain.

Ghalatfehmi 3. FPA ek tax hai

Nahi. Yeh fuel ka farq hai jo pass through kiya jata hai. Tax ki tarah is par sarkar ki aamdani nahi banti. Aur yeh negative bhi ho sakta hai, jo koi tax nahi kar sakta.

Ghalatfehmi 4. FPA har mahina barhta hai

Nahi. 2026 mein yeh do baar negative hua. January ke bills par Rs 0.93 ka credit mila. Rate har mahine reference aur actual cost ke farq par munhasir hai.

Ghalatfehmi 5. Prepaid consumers ko bhi FPA lagta hai

Nahi. Har 2026 ke notification mein saaf likha hai ke prepaid tariff walon par FPA nafiz nahi hoga. Un ka FPA zero hai.

Sources

Sahi maloomat kahan se milti hai

FPA ek aisi cheez hai jis par har mahina naye dawe aate hain. Is liye hamesha sarkari zariye se tasdeeq karein.

  • Ministry of Energy (power.gov.pk). FPA ki sarkari tareef aur tax table yahan shaya hoti hai.
  • NEPRA (nepra.org.pk). Har mahine ka FCA notification S.R.O. number ke sath yahan milta hai.
  • Aap ki apni bill. Sab se aakhiri sanad. Jo figure bill par likha hai, wahi lagoo hai.

Kisi bhi calculator ya blog ko aakhiri sanad na banayein. Un ke figures purane ho sakte hain. NEPRA ka naya S.R.O. hamesha purane andazon ko mansookh kar deta hai.

NEPRA kaise kaam karta hai, yeh samajhne ke liye hamari NEPRA tariff guide dekhein.

Checklist

FPA samajhne ki aakhri checklist

  • FPA fuel ka farq hai, tax nahi. Isay tax wale block mein dekh kar dhoka na khayein.
  • FPA aur FCA ek hi cheez hain. Do alag charges nahi.
  • Rate ek hi qaumi hai. DISCO ke hisaab se nahi badalta.
  • Aap ka FPA aap ke units par munhasir hai. Rate sab ka ek hai.
  • Lifeline, EV charging aur prepaid consumers ka FPA zero hai.
  • Bill ki line us fuel ki hoti hai jo do mahine pehle jala tha.
  • Negative FPA credit hai. Bill kam hota hai, ghabrayein nahi.
  • Har mahine ka rate naye S.R.O. se tabdeel hota hai. Purane figures purane ho jate hain.
FAQs

Aksar poochhe jane wale sawalat

FPA aur FCA mein kya farq hai?

Koi farq nahi. Yeh ek hi charge ke do naam hain. NEPRA apne notifications mein Fuel Charges Adjustment (FCA) likhta hai, jabke aksar bills aur DISCO software par Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA) chapta hai. Dono ka matlab ek hai, fuel ka reference aur actual cost ke darmiyan ka farq.

Is waqt FPA kitna hai?

October 2026 ke bills ke liye FPA Rs 1.1086 per unit hai. Yeh August 2026 ke fuel ke liye hai aur S.R.O. 1754(I)/2026 ke tehat 7 October 2026 ko notify hua. Is ke sath QTA Rs 0.5194 per unit bhi chal raha hai. Is tarah kul Rs 1.628 per unit banta hai. Agla rate NEPRA ke agle notification par munhasir hoga.

Kya har DISCO ka FPA rate alag hota hai?

Nahi. NEPRA ek hi qaumi rate notify karta hai jo tamam ex-WAPDA DISCOs aur K-Electric par lagoo hota hai. Jo farq nazar aata hai woh units ka hota hai. 500 units istemal karne wale ka FPA 200 units wale se zyada hoga, lekin per unit rate dono ka bilkul ek hai.

Kya FPA ek tax hai?

Nahi. FPA ek cost pass-through hai. Sarkar is se koi aamdani nahi kamati. Yeh woh farq hai jo bijli banane walon ko fuel ka asal mein pada, jo tariff mein lagaaye gaye andaze se zyada ya kam ho. Tax ke baraks, FPA negative bhi ho sakta hai, aur tab yeh aap ke bill se kat-ta hai.

Prepaid meter walon ka FPA kitna hota hai?

Zero. Har NEPRA FCA notification mein saaf likha hota hai ke prepaid tariff ka intekhab karne wale tamam consumers par yeh adjustment nafiz nahi hoga. Isi tarah lifeline consumers aur electric vehicle charging stations ko bhi chhoot hai.

Mera FPA parosi se zyada kyun hai?

Sab se aam wajah units ka farq hai. FPA rate sab ke liye ek hai, lekin raqam units ke hisaab se lagti hai. Doosri wajoohat mein billing date ka farq shamil hai, kyun ke agar aap ka bill notification se pehle chhap gaya ho to adjustment agle mahine lagta hai.

Negative FPA ka kya matlab hai?

Is ka matlab hai ke us mahine asal fuel cost tariff mein lagaaye gaye andaze se kam thi. Farq aap ko wapas diya jata hai. Bill par yeh bracket mein ya minus ke sath chapta hai, jaise (Rs 93). Yeh aap ka credit hai, koi ghalti nahi. 2026 mein January ke bills par Rs 0.93 per unit ka negative adjustment laga tha.

Next step

Ab apna FPA khud check karein

FPA samajhne ka faida tab hai jab aap isay apne hi bill par verify karein. Apni taaza duplicate bill kholein, FPA ki line dhoondhein, aur apne units se rate multiply kar ke dekhein.

Agar koi farq hai, to pehle billing month check karein. Ho sakta hai aap ka bill naye notification se pehle chhapa ho.

Apni bill abhi check karein aur dekhein ke FPA ki line aap ke hisaab se milti hai ya nahi.
