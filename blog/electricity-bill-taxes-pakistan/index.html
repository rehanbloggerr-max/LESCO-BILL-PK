Quick answer

Which taxes sit on an electricity bill?

A Pakistani electricity bill carries six government levies. GST is the largest, at 17% of the electricity charges. Electricity duty adds 1 to 1.5%. Income tax takes 7.5% from non-filers above Rs 25,000.

Three smaller lines follow them. The PTV licence fee is Rs 35 for homes. Further tax is 3%. Extra tax applies to unregistered businesses.

GST energy charge par lagta hai. Is mein woh adjustments bhi shamil hain jo energy charge ke saath aate hain. TV fee aur meter rent par GST nahi lagta. Electricity duty soobai hai, is liye Punjab aur Sindh ki rate mein farq ho sakta hai. Income tax sab se zyada ghalatfehmi wali line hai.

Ghar ke connection par yeh sirf non-filers par lagti hai. Filers ke liye domestic bill par yeh bilkul zero hai.

Sabse zaroori baat yeh hai ke income tax adjustable hai. Isay annual return mein claim kiya ja sakta hai. Bahut log claim karte hi nahi.

Read the block

What each line in the tax block means

GST, sabse badi line

General Sales Tax aap ke bill ka sabse bada tax hai. Sales Tax Act 1990 ke tehat lagta hai. Ministry of Energy ke published table ke mutabiq rate 17 feesad hai.

Yeh energy charge par lagta hai. Is mein woh adjustments bhi shamil hain jo energy charge ke saath aate hain. TV licence fee aur meter rent par GST nahi lagta.

Protected consumers ke liye bhi koi chhoot nahi hai. Un ki subsidized rate par bhi GST lagta hai.

Electricity duty, soobai tax

Electricity duty ek soobai tax hai. Yeh variable charges ke 1 se 1.5 feesad ke darmiyan hota hai. Har sooba apni rate rakhta hai.

Yeh GST se alag hai. GST ke baad lagta hai. Ziyada tar gharon ke liye yeh chhoti line hoti hai. Lekin bill ke saath barh jati hai.

Income tax, section 235

Income Tax Ordinance 2001 ki section 235 ke tehat DISCO aap ke bill se withholding tax kat-ta hai. Yeh raqam FBR ko jati hai.

Ghar ke connection par yeh sirf non-filers par lagti hai. Sirf jab bill Rs 25,000 ya us se zyada ho. Rate 7.5 feesad hai poore bill par.

Filers ke liye domestic connection par yeh zero hai. Commercial aur industrial ke liye alag slabs hain. Woh neeche table mein hain.

PTV licence fee

Yeh Pakistan Television ke liye hai. Bill ke zariye wasool kiya jata hai. Ministry of Energy ke mutabiq gharon ke liye Rs 35 hai.

Commercial connections ke liye Rs 60 mahana hai. Aap ke paas TV ho ya na ho, yeh lagta hai. Is par GST nahi lagta.

Further tax aur extra tax

Further tax 3 feesad hai. Yeh un par lagta hai jin ke paas Sales Tax Return Number nahi hai. Ghar, zarai, bulk aur street light connections is se mustasna hain.

Extra tax commercial aur industrial consumers par lagta hai. Woh log jin ka naam FBR ke Active Taxpayers List mein nahi. Yeh 5 se 17 feesad ke darmiyan hota hai.

Rate bill ki raqam ke hisaab se badalti hai.

Step by step

How to read the tax block on your own bill

  1. Apni latest duplicate bill kholein ya PITC se download karein.
  2. Sab se upar energy charge aur units ki line dhoondhein.
  3. Us ke neechay FPA aur QTA ki lines dekhein, yeh negative bhi ho sakti hain.
  4. Phir taxes wala block dhoondhein, jahan GST aur duty likha hota hai.
  5. Income tax ki line check karein, agar zero hai to aap filer hain.
  6. PTV fee aur meter rent ki alag lines bhi neechay hoti hain.

Har DISCO ka bill thora alag dikhta hai. Lekin block ki tarteeb lagbhag ek jaisi hai. Energy charge sab se pehle aata hai, phir adjustments, phir taxes, phir fixed lines.

Ek baar block samajh aa jaye to aap har mahine usay teen minute mein parh lein ge.

Agar aap ko apni bill nahi mili to bill na aane wali guide dekh lein. Duplicate bill PITC se foran mil jati hai.

Reference number ka matlab samajhna ho to reference number aur customer ID ka farq wali guide madad kare gi.

The GST confusion

Is GST on electricity 17% or 18%?

Sarkari table kya kehta hai

Ministry of Energy apni website par likhta hai ke GST 17 feesad hai. Yeh wohi department hai jo NEPRA ke tariff ko notify karta hai. Is liye sarkari hawala 17 feesad ka hai.

Lekin Pakistan ka aam sales tax rate 18 feesad hai. Isi wajah se confusion paida hoti hai. Donon numbers sahi jagahon par lagu hote hain.

18 feesad kahan se aata hai

Bahut se online calculators standard rate 18 feesad lagate hain. Kuch companies bhi yahi likhti hain. K-Electric ki apni published guidance mein bhi 18 feesad hai.

Donon numbers internet par ghoom rahe hain. Is liye kisi bhi website ko aakhiri sanad mat samjhein. Chahe woh humari hi kyun na ho.

Aap ke bill par kya chhapa hai

Aakhiri faisla aap ke apne bill ki GST line karti hai. Wohi raqam wusool ki jati hai. Bill par jo likha hai, wohi sahi hai.

Agar aap ko lagta hai ke ghalat rate lagaya gaya hai, to DISCO ke revenue office se poochhein. Woh wazahat karne ke paaband hain.

Is farq ka asar

Ek feesad ka farq chhota lagta hai. Lekin bade bill par yeh bara ho jata hai. Tees hazaar ke energy charge par farq teen sau rupaye hai.

Saal bhar mein yeh kuch hazaar rupaye ban jate hain. Is liye apne bill ki GST line khud parhna behtar hai.

Hum kya mashwara dete hain

Apne bill ka GST number khud parhein. Usay apne hisaab mein daalein. Kisi website ke number par bharosa na karein.

Agar aap ke bill ka number is guide se different hai, to aap ka bill sahi hai. Hum sirf sarkari table ka hawala de rahe hain.

Income tax

Section 235 slabs in plain words

Ghar, non filer, Rs 25,000 se kam

Income tax zero hai. Rs 25,000 se kam ke mahane bill par koi withholding nahi hota. Ziyada tar Pakistani gharon ke liye yahi halat hai.

Ghar, non filer, Rs 25,000 ya zyada

7.5 feesad poore bill par lagta hai. Tees hazaar ke bill par yeh Rs 2,250 hai, chalis hazaar par Rs 3,000 mahana. Saal bhar mein Rs 36,000.

Ghar, filer

Zero hai. Domestic connection par filers is clause se bahar hain. Bill kitna bhi bara ho. Yeh wajah hai ke ATL mein aana sabse badi bachat hai.

Commercial, Rs 500 tak

Zero hai. Chhote business bills par koi income tax nahi lagta.

Commercial, Rs 501 se Rs 20,000

10 feesad bill par lagta hai. Rs 10,000 ke commercial bill par yeh Rs 1,000 hai.

Commercial, Rs 20,000 se zyada

Rs 1,950 ke sath izafi raqam par 12 feesad lagta hai. Industrial connections ke liye izafi raqam par rate sirf 5 feesad hai. Yeh unhen bari raahat deti hai.

Get it back

How to claim the income tax back

Yeh woh hissa hai jahan aksar paisa zaya hota hai. Withholding tax adjustable hota hai, matlab aap isay wapas le sakte hain.

  1. Saal ke barah mahine ki bills mahfooz rakhein ya PDF download kar lein.
  2. Har bill ki income tax line alag se note karein.
  3. Saal ki kul raqam jama karein, yeh aap ka deducted tax hai.
  4. FBR ke IRIS portal par apna account kholein ya login karein.
  5. Annual income tax return kholein, chahe aap ki income taxable na ho.
  6. Tax deducted section mein electricity bills wali raqam daalein.
  7. Bills ki PDFs sath attach karein jahan form ijazat de.
  8. Return submit karein aur acknowledgement receipt mahfooz rakhein.

Jo log nil return file karte hain, un ke liye bhi yeh claim mumkin hai. Income zero hone par bhi deducted tax wapas mil sakta hai.

Har saal bari raqam claim kiye bagair reh jati hai. Pakistan Today ki reporting ke mutabiq yeh Rs 400 se 500 arab rupaye tak hai.

Yeh paisa sarkari khazane mein rehta hai. Haqdaaron tak nahi pohnchta.

ATL route

How to get on the Active Taxpayers List

ATL mein aana is tax se bachne ka sabse seedha tareeqa hai. Is ke liye bari income zaroori nahi.

  1. FBR ke IRIS portal par apne CNIC se registration karein.
  2. Agar pehle kabhi file nahi kiya to NTN ke liye apply karein.
  3. Apni maloomat, bank account aur mobile number verify karein.
  4. Annual return file karein, agar income kam hai to nil return.
  5. FBR ke ATL search par apna CNIC check karein.
  6. Jab naam ATL mein aa jaye to DISCO ke revenue office ko agah karein.

Ek baar ATL mein naam aa jaye to agle billing cycle se domestic income tax line zero ho jani chahiye. Agar nahi hoti to revenue office se correction likhwana zaroori hai.

Nil return file karna muft hai. Online bhi hota hai.

Kai log isay chhor dete hain, kyun ke unhen lagta hai ke un ki income taxable nahi hai. Yahi wajah hai ke un se tax kat-ta rehta hai.

Business bills

Commercial and industrial connections

Business connections par tax ka bojh kaafi alag hota hai. In cheezon ka khayal rakhein.

  • Sales tax 5 feesad Rs 20,000 tak ke bill par, aur 7.5 feesad us se zyada par.
  • Extra tax 5 se 17 feesad, agar aap ATL mein nahi hain.
  • Income tax 10 feesad Rs 501 se Rs 20,000 tak ke bills par.
  • Rs 20,000 se zyada par Rs 1,950 ke sath 12 feesad commercial ke liye.
  • Industrial ke liye izafi raqam par sirf 5 feesad.
  • PTV fee Rs 60 mahana bajaye Rs 35 ke.

Industrial connections ko income tax mein bari raahat milti hai. Izafi raqam par rate sirf 5 feesad hai, bajaye 12 feesad ke. Bari factory bills mein yeh farq dalti hai.

Agar aap ka business registered hai to extra tax se bachne ka asan tareeqa hai: ATL mein aana. Yeh sales tax registration se alag cheez hai.

Bahut se chhote dukandar apni connection ka category ghalat hone ki wajah se domestic rate kho dete hain. Apne bill ka tariff category zaroor check karein.

Karachi

What changes for K-Electric consumers

K-Electric Karachi mein apna alag system chalata hai. Bunyaad wohi hai: GST, electricity duty aur income tax. Rates alag ho sakte hain.

KE ki apni published guidance ke mutabiq residential bills par income tax 7.5 feesad hai. Yeh sirf un customers par lagta hai jo FBR ke Active Taxpayers List mein nahi hain.

GST ki rate KE ke apne material mein 18 feesad batayi gayi hai. Is liye Karachi ke consumers ke liye aakhiri sanad un ki apni KE bill hai.

Kisi DISCO ke rate ko KE par lagu mat samjhein. Na hi KE ke rate ko kisi DISCO par.

Har sawal ka jawab KE ke apne customer service se lein, kyun ke wohi aap ki bill jari karta hai.

Not really taxes

Surcharges people mistake for tax

Fuel price adjustment

FPA har mahine NEPRA notify karta hai. Yeh pichhle mahine ke asli fuel cost ka farq hai. Positive bhi ho sakta hai. Negative bhi.

Yeh tax nahi hai. Yeh asli lagat ki adjustment hai. Is par GST lagta hai, is liye bill ko do tarah se barhata hai.

Quarterly tariff adjustment

QTA har teen mahine baad aata hai. Is mein power purchase price ka farq shamil hota hai. Capacity payments aur transmission losses bhi isi mein hain.

Kuch quarters mein yeh credit hota hai. Matlab bill kam hota hai. June se August 2026 ke doran yeh Rs 1.9857 feesad per unit ka credit tha.

Financing cost surcharge

Financing cost surcharge circular debt ki qiston ke liye hai. Is ki rate par ziyada tar guides aapas mein nahi miltin.

Ministry of Energy ke published table ke mutabiq yeh Rs 0.43 per kWh hai. Aam taur par isay Rs 3.23 per unit ke hisaab se pesh kiya jata hai.

Is farq ki wajah se hum isay apne hisaab mein shamil nahi karte. Apne bill ki line zaroor dekhein.

Neelum Jhelum surcharge

Yeh Neelum Jhelum project ke liye ek chhoti surcharge hai. Aksar Rs 0.10 se Rs 1.00 per unit ke darmiyan hoti hai.

Yeh bhi tax nahi hai. Yeh ek project levy hai, aur is par GST lagta hai.

Meter rent

Meter rent DISCO ke meter ka mahana kiraya hai. Is ka sarkari naam minimum monthly customer charge hai. NEPRA ke tariff ke mutabiq ghar ke single phase connection par Rs 75 mahana aur three phase par Rs 150 mahana hai. Ye tab bhi lagta hai jab aap ne koi unit istemal na ki ho.

Bahut se calculators isay Rs 20 se Rs 50 ke darmiyan likhte hain. Ye ghalat hai. IESCO, MEPCO, HAZECO aur K-Electric ki tarif chek karein, sab Rs 75 aur Rs 150 hi kehti hain.

Ek zaroori baat. February 2026 se jin connections par naya per-kW fixed charge lagta hai, un par ye minimum charge lagti hi nahi. NEPRA ke notification mein saaf likha hai ke jahan fixed charges nafiz hon, wahan minimum charges nafiz nahi hongi. Is liye aaj kal zyadatar gharon ke bill par meter rent ki line nazar hi nahi aati.

Agar aap ne khud ka meter lagaya hai to yeh nahi lagni chahiye. Ghalat charge hone par revenue office se correction karwaein.

Worked maths

A 300-unit bill, taxed line by line

Misaal ka bill

Hum 300 units ka ghar ka bill lete hain: LESCO, unprotected, June 2026. Is misaal mein sirf woh lines hain jin ki rate par sab muttafiq hain. Financing cost surcharge shamil nahi. Upar wale section mein wajah likhi hai.

Energy charge

300 units ka slab rate Rs 33.10 per unit hai. Is hisaab se 300 x 33.10 = Rs 9,930 banta hai. Yeh sabse badi line hai. Agar aap 301 se 400 units ke slab mein hote to rate Rs 36.46 hota. Slab poori consumption par lagta hai.

Quarterly adjustment

June se August 2026 ke liye QTA ek credit tha. Is hisaab se 300 x Rs 1.9857 = Rs 596 ki kami hoti hai. Negative adjustment bill ko kam karta hai, aur har quarter yeh badalta hai.

GST 17 feesad

Energy charge Rs 9,930 hai. Is mein se QTA credit Rs 596 ghata kar Rs 9,334 banta hai. GST 17 feesad = Rs 1,587. Yahan woh ek feesad ka farq dikhta hai: 18 feesad par yeh Rs 1,680 hota. Matlab Rs 93 ka farq.

Electricity duty aur TV fee

Electricity duty energy charge par 1.5 feesad hai, yani Rs 9,930 ka 1.5 feesad = Rs 149. TV licence fee Rs 35 hai. Duty GST ke baad alag se lagti hai, aur is ke upar GST nahi lagta.

Kul bill aur income tax

Rs 9,334 aur GST Rs 1,587 milayen. Duty Rs 149 aur TV fee Rs 35 bhi joren. Kul bill Rs 11,105 banta hai. Rs 25,000 se kam hone par income tax zero hai. Agar yahi bill Rs 30,000 ka hota to non filer par Rs 2,250 lagta, filer par zero. Yeh Rs 2,250 wapas bhi liya ja sakta hai.

Upar claim wala section dekhein.

Avoid mistakes

Mistakes that cost real money

Income tax ko cost samajhna

Log isay bijli ki lagat samajh kar pareshan hote hain. Haqeeqat mein yeh advance tax hai. Isay annual return mein claim karein.

Filer hone ke bawajood tax bharna

Agar aap ka CNIC ATL mein nahi hai to aap filer nahi maane jate. Har saal ATL status check karein. Yeh har saal renew hota hai.

Connection kisi aur ke naam par

Bahut se gharon mein connection walid ya kiraye dar ke CNIC par hota hai. Tax usi shakhs ke status ke hisaab se lagta hai. Woh shakhs jo record mein hai.

GST rate par behas karna

17 ya 18 feesad par behas se kuch nahi badalta. Apne bill ki line parhein. Wohi raqam wusool hoti hai.

Surcharge ko tax kehna

FPA, QTA aur financing cost surcharge tax nahi hain. In ki bunyaad alag hai. Inhein tax samajh kar complain karna bekaar jata hai.

Bills na sambhalna

Claim ke liye barah mahine ki bills chahiye hoti hain. Jo log bills phenk dete hain, woh apna tax wapas nahi le sakte.

Quick answers

FAQs about electricity bill taxes

What taxes are charged on an electricity bill in Pakistan?

A Pakistani electricity bill can carry six separate government levies. GST is the biggest, at 17% of the electricity charges. Provincial electricity duty adds 1 to 1.5%. Withholding income tax falls under section 235 of the Income Tax Ordinance. The PTV licence fee is Rs 35 for homes. Further tax is 3%. Extra tax hits unregistered commercial users. The Ministry of Energy publishes this whole table on its own website.

Is GST on electricity 17% or 18%?

The Ministry of Energy tax table says 17% of the electricity bill. Pakistan standard sales tax rate is 18%. Many calculators therefore quote the higher figure. Some companies apply the standard rate instead of the reduced one. Your own bill settles it. Read the figure on the bill, not on any website. This one included.

Why is income tax charged on my electricity bill?

It is withholding tax under section 235 of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. Your DISCO collects it for the FBR. Domestic consumers outside the Active Taxpayers List pay 7.5% once a monthly bill reaches Rs 25,000. Filers pay nothing on a domestic connection. The amount stays adjustable against your annual income tax.

I am a filer, so why am I still being charged income tax?

Three reasons are common. Your CNIC is missing from the FBR Active Taxpayers List for the current year. The connection sits under another person CNIC. Or the bill is commercial rather than domestic. Check your status on the FBR ATL search, then ask your DISCO revenue office to correct the category.

What is the Rs 35 PTV licence fee on my bill?

It is a statutory levy collected through electricity bills for Pakistan Television. The Ministry of Energy lists it at Rs 35 a month for domestic consumers and Rs 60 for commercial. It applies whether or not you own a television. No GST is charged on top of it.

Do commercial and industrial connections pay different taxes?

Yes. Commercial consumers pay sales tax at 5% on bills up to Rs 20,000. Above the limit the rate is 7.5%. Unregistered commercial and industrial users pay extra tax of 5 to 17%. Income tax runs at 10% on bills from Rs 501 to Rs 20,000. Above Rs 20,000 it is Rs 1,950 plus 12%. For industrial users the extra rate is only 5%.

How do I stop paying the 7.5% income tax?

Join the FBR Active Taxpayers List by filing a return, even a nil return. Filers sit outside this withholding clause on domestic connections. Once your CNIC appears on the ATL, tell your DISCO revenue office and keep the bills. Tax already deducted can be claimed in your annual return.
