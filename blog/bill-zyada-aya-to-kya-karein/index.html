Bijli ka bill zyada aya to kya karein

Bill khola. Raqam dekhi. Dil baith gaya. Aap akele nahi hain. Har saal lakhon logon ke sath ye hota hai. Achhi khabar ye hai ke NEPRA ne poori kitab likhi hai. Us mein saaf likha hai ke kaunsi shikayat kitne din mein mukammal honi chahiye.

Bill zyada aane par log do kaam karte hain. Ya to ghabra kar poora bharr dete hain, ya office ke chakkar lagate rehte hain. Dono ghalat hain.

Haqeeqat ye hai. NEPRA ne ek kitab banayi hai. Isay Consumer Service Manual kehte hain. Ye 2025 mein nayi surat mein chhapi, NEPRA ne sab DISCOs ko bheji. Har DISCO ko hukm hai ke isay apni website par lagayein.

Is kitab mein har shikayat ke liye waqt likha hai. Ghalat reading ho to 7 din. Late payment ka jurmana ghalat laga ho to 3 din. Detection bill ho to 15 din, ye sab kuch likha hai.

Masla sirf itna hai. Ye kitab English mein hai. Zyada logon ne isay padha hi nahi. Hum ne ye kaam aap ke liye kar diya hai.

Quick answer

Pehle din kya karein

Sab se pehle paani piyein aur ghabrana chhor dein. Phir meter par jayein. Wahan jo number chal raha hai, usay note kar lein.

Ab bill uthayein. Us par 'Present Reading' likha hota hai. Apni note ki hui reading se milayein. Farq nikal aayega.

Reading bill se zyada hai? To meter ki wazeh tasweer lein, jis mein number saaf padha ja sake. Aaj ki tareekh sath rakhein.

Phir DISCO ko shikayat karein. Likhit mein. Stamp lagwa kar copy apne paas rakhein. Zabani shikayat ka koi record nahi banta.

  1. Meter ke paas jayein. Display par jo number hai, woh note kar lein.
  2. Bill par 'Present Reading' dhoondhein. Ye upar ya beech mein likhi hoti hai.
  3. Dono ka farq nikal lein. Farq bill ke units se zyada hai to aage barhein.
  4. Meter ki wazeh tasweer lein aur number saaf padha ja sake.
  5. Pichhle 6 mahine ke bill nikal lein. Muqabla karne ke liye.
  6. Nearest DISCO office jayein, ya CCMS par online shikayat karein.
  7. Stamped copy lein. Us par tareekh likhi honi chahiye.
Pehle ye

Shuru karne se pehle chhe kaam

Shikayat likhne se pehle kuch kaam ho jane chahiye. Ye waqt bachayenge. Baar baar office jane se bachayenge.

Apna reference number sambhal lein. Ye 14 digits ka hota hai aur har purane bill par chhapa hota hai. Phone mein save kar lein.

Pichhle chhe mahine ke bill nikal lein. Kaghzi ho ya online. In ke baghair muqabla nahi ho sakta.

Ek register rakhein. Har call ki tareekh likhein aur har office jane ka din likhein. Ye aakhir mein kaam aayega.

Step 1

Reading ko bill se milayein

Ye sab se aasan kaam hai aur is mein koi kharcha nahi. Koi office jana nahi. Sirf aankhon se dekhna hai.

Meter par 'Present Reading' likha hota hai aur bill par bhi yahi lafz hote hain. Dono ko milana hai.

Farq samajh lijiye. Meter aaj 5420 chal raha hai. Bill par 5400 likha hai. Ab to 20 units ka hi farq hai.

Bill par 5450 likha hai? To 30 units zyada charge hue. Ye ghalat hai. Shikayat ka pehla saboot yahi hai.

Kabhi meter band padha hota hai aur display kharaab ho sakti hai. Aisi surat mein tasweer lein. Usay shikayat ke sath lagayein.

Step 2

Pro-rata ka hisaab lagayein

Ye cheez lakhon logon ko pata hi nahi aur nEPRA ka rule bahut wazeh hai. Reading ka daura 30 din ka hona chahiye.

Kabhi reading 33 din baad hoti hai. Kabhi 35 din baad. Aisi surat mein DISCO ko poora bill nahi karna chahiye. Pro-rata karna zaroori hai.

Ise samjhiye. Aap ne 33 din mein 208 units chalaye. Hisaab ye hoga. 208 divided by 33, times 30. Jawab 189 units.

Ab 189 units hi is mahine charge honge aur baqi 19 units agle bill mein jayenge. Aap ka protected status bhi barqarar rahega.

SuratMeterDinBill hongeAgle bill
Lifeline, 50 tak54 units3349 units5 units
Lifeline, 51 se 100109 units3399 units10 units
Protected, 200 tak208 units33189 units19 units
Slab 201 se 300309 units33281 units28 units

Agle bill mein ye units 'carry forwarded' likh kar aayenge. Is mahine ke bill par 'outstanding units' alag se likhe honge.

Step 3

Bijli ka bill zyada aya to kya karein: DISCO shikayat

Ab waqt hai shikayat ka. Pehla darwaza DISCO ka hai. NEPRA ke rules isi tarteeb ko kehte hain.

Teen raaste hain aur online CCMS portal. Helpline 118. Ya khud office ja kar One Window.

Sab se behtar raasta likhit hai. Office jayein. Ek darkhwast likhein. Doosri copy par stamp lagwa lein.

Stamp ke sath tareekh aati hai. Ye tareekh sab se qeemti cheez hai aur isi se aap aage ja sakte hain.

  1. Apne DISCO ka naam confirm karein aur lESCO, IESCO, MEPCO, K-Electric, waghaira.
  2. CCMS portal kholein aur reference number dalen. OTP aayega.
  3. Shikayat ki qism chunein aur billing complaint. Ya meter reading.
  4. Saboot lagayein aur meter ki tasweer. Purane bill. Sab pdf ya jpg mein.
  5. Neem mein apni baat likhein aur tareekhen saaf likhein. Raqam likhein.
  6. Complaint number mil jayega. Usay register mein likh lein.
  7. Office gaye hain to stamped copy zaroor lein. Warna shikayat hui hi nahi.

Ek zaroori baat. Jo raqam aap manzoor karte hain, woh foran ada kar dein. Sirf itna. Jis hisse par aitraz hai, usi ki baat karein, poori raqam par nahi.

Poori raqam roak kar baithna nuksan karta hai. Baqi raqam par connection katwa sakte hain, phir baat aap ke khilaf jayegi. Is liye ada kar dein.

Step 4

Meter ka test karwayein

Meter tez chal raha ho to kya karein? NEPRA ne is ka bhi tareeqa likha hai. Isay meter challenge kehte hain.

Aap DISCO ko likh kar kahein ke mera meter theek se check kiya jaye. DISCO ek demand notice bhejega, us mein fee likhi hogi.

Fee kitni hai? Single phase ke liye 500 rupaye aur three phase ke liye 1000 rupaye. Ye NEPRA ne mukarrar ki hai.

Fee jama karwayein. Ab DISCO ko 7 working days mein test karna hoga. Aap ko bulaya jayega aur aap ki maujoodgi mein check hoga.

Ab do natije ho sakte hain. Meter tez nikla to naya meter DISCO ke paisay se. Sath hi do pichhle bill cycles ka credit.

Meter sust nikla? Phir bhi DISCO khud kharcha uthayega. Lekin do cycles ki kami aap se li ja sakti hai.

WajahKharcha kaun dega
Meter kharaab, jal gaya, display washDISCO
Budhape ki wajah se sustDISCO
Meter tez chal raha ho, kisi bhi wajah seDISCO
Overload ya wiring ki ghalti se kharaabConsumer
Meter ghaib ho gaya, ghar mein laga thaConsumer
Meter pole par tha, accident huaDISCO

Ghar khali tha aur average bill aaya? Saboot dein. NEPRA kehta hai sirf minimum charges lene honge.

Step 5

Electric Inspector se rabta karein

DISCO ne baat na mani? Ab aap ko ek bada haq milta hai. Usay Electric Inspector kehte hain aur purane lafz hain POI.

Ye DISCO ka mulazim nahi hai. Ye aap ki taraf se meter ki jaanch karta hai. Is liye is par bharosa zyada hota hai.

Tareeqa ye hai. Aap Electric Inspector ko darkhwast dein. Sath hi DISCO ko likhit notice bhejein.

Notice mein likhein. Mein ne Electric Inspector se checking ki darkhwast ki hai. Ab DISCO ka meter hatana band ho jata hai.

Ye NEPRA ka seedha hukm hai. Inspector test kar le, tab tak meter wahin rahega aur test ke waqt aap bhi maujood honge.

Inspector ko ek mahine mein test karna hota hai. Meter tez ya sust nikla to foran badla jayega.

Meter maujood na ho to do billing cycles ka waqt mil sakta hai. Hisaab sirf do mahine tak hoga. Is se zyada nahi.

Electric Inspector ka pata apne DISCO office se poochh lein. Ya apne ilaqe ke energy department se aur hum ne koi number tasdeeq nahi kiya.

Step 6

Bijli ka bill zyada aya to kya karein: NEPRA tak

NEPRA sab se bada darwaza hai. Lekin isay aakhir mein khata khat karna chahiye aur pehle DISCO ko mauqa dena zaroori hai.

NEPRA ka online system hai. Usay Complaint Management System kehte hain aur is par shikayat bhi darj hogi. Status bhi nazar aayega.

Teen cheezein aap ko roak sakti hain. Pehli ye ke shikayat ek saal se purani na ho. Doosri, DISCO ko shikayat ki ho aur teesri, affidavit dena hoga.

Ek saal purani shikayat? System foran mana kar deta hai. Aisi surat mein apni darkhwast DG Consumer Affairs NEPRA ko likh kar bhejein. Sirf likhit mein, online form kaam nahi aata.

  1. Nearest NEPRA office chunein. Das shehron mein hain, jin mein Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta shamil hain.
  2. Reference number, CNIC, mobile number tayyar rakhein. Sab zaroori hain.
  3. Pichhli DISCO shikayat ki copy lagayein. Us ka jawab ho to woh bhi.
  4. Jis bill par aitraz hai, us ki copy lagayein. Saaf ho.
  5. Files pdf ya jpg hone chahiye. Har file 2.5 MB se chhoti ho.
  6. Affidavit par tick karein. Is ke baghair form aage nahi jata.
  7. Submit kar dein aur complaint number mil jayega. Usay sambhal lein.

Fon par baat karni ho to 051-2013200 hai. Ye NEPRA head office aur DG Consumer Affairs, dono ka number hai.

Email hai cad@nepra.org.pk. Waqt subah 9 se sham 5 tak hai. Peer se Jumma tak.

NEPRA ki 'Asaan Approach' app bhi hai aur is mein shikayat darj karein. Us ka status bhi dekhein. Free hai.

Step 7

Wafaqi Mohtasib, aakhri darwaza

Sab kuch kar ke bhi insaaf na mile? Aakhri darwaza Wafaqi Mohtasib hai. Isay Federal Ombudsman bhi kehte hain.

Ye sarkari idara hai aur har shehri ki shikayat sunta hai. Sarkari idaron ke khilaf. Bijli ki companies bhi is mein aati hain.

NEPRA ke apne khaton mein likha hota hai. Masla hal na ho to Wafaqi Mohtasib se rabta kar sakte hain.

Hum ne is ka pata official website se tasdeeq nahi kiya. Website khul nahi rahi thi. Is liye hum number nahi likh rahe.

Aap ka DISCO office is ka pata bata sakta hai. Ya phir kisi wakil se poochh lein. Ghalat number par waqt zaya na karein.

Ek baat yaad rakhein. Wafaqi Mohtasib tabhi sunega, jab aap ne pehle ke sab darwaze khat khate kiye hon.

Detection bill

Detection bill ki asal hadd

Kabhi bill mein 'Detection' likha aata hai. Raqam bhaari hoti hai. Log ghabra jate hain aur lekin is ki bhi hadd hai.

NEPRA ke rule ke mutabiq detection bill ki muddat chhe mahine hai. Registered consumer ke liye. Yani aap purane consumer hain to chhe mahine.

Unregistered ke liye baarah mahine. Lekin registered ke liye chhe se zyada nahi. Ye sab se badi baat hai.

Isay samjhiye. Aap ke ghar mein bijli chori ka ilzaam laga. DISCO ne paanch saal ka hisaab laga diya. Ye ghalat hai.

Aap ke hisaab ka tareeqa bhi tay hai. Pehle pichhla record dekha jayega. Purani consumption ke hisaab se. Record na ho to aane wale mahinon ka muqabla.

Yani jo aap ne pichhle barah mahine mein chalaya, usi ke hisaab se. Mann maani raqam nahi lag sakti.

Detection bill par faisla 15 din mein hona chahiye. Ye bhi NEPRA ne likha hai.

Ghalatfehmiyan

Chhe baatein jo log ghalat samajhte hain

Internet par bijli ke bill ke bare mein bahut kuch likha hai. Zyada tar ghalat hai. Ye chhe baatein sab se zyada phaili hui hain.

Pehli. NEPRA ka helpline 1915 hai. Bilkul ghalat. 1915 to Hindustan ka number hai. Wahan ki sarkar chala rahi hai aur pakistan se is ka koi lena dena nahi.

Doosri. Detection bill jitna marzi aa sakta hai. Ghalat. Registered consumer ke liye chhe mahine ki hadd hai.

Teesri. Meter tez nikle to naya meter apne paisay se lena padega. Ghalat. Tezi kisi bhi wajah se ho, kharcha DISCO ka hai.

Chauthi. Shikayat ka koi waqt muqarrar nahi. Ghalat. 7 din se 30 din tak sab likha hai.

Panchwin. Reading 35 din ki ho to poora bill dena padega. Ghalat. Pro-rata karna zaroori hai.

Chhathi. Zabani keh dena kaafi hai. Hargiz nahi. Likhat mein shikayat karein aur stamped copy rakhein.

Khaas surat

Ghar khali tha, phir bhi bill aaya

Ye masla bahut aam hai. Aap do mahine bahar gaye aur ghar band pada raha. Phir bhi bill aata raha aur ab kya karein?

Pehle farq samajh lijiye. Meter ki reading na ho to DISCO andaze se bill banata hai. Isay average billing kehte hain.

Naye rule mein ek raahat hai. Aap saboot dein ke ghar khali tha aur phir DISCO sirf minimum charges le sakta hai.

Saboot kya hoga aur safar ka ticket. Karaye ka kaghz. Koi bhi saboot jis se saaf ho ke bijli chali hi nahi.

Ek aur baat janiye. Ghar band hone par reader ko meter tak pohanch mushkil hoti hai. Is ka bhi rule hai.

NEPRA kehta hai ke do mahine tak ek appointment kaafi hai. Us ke baad nayi appointment leni padegi, reading update karne ke liye.

Lekin isay haath se na jane dein. Office ko pehle hi likh dein. Ghar khali hai, aap reading ke liye tashreef la sakte hain.

K-Electric

Karachi walon ke liye alag baat

Karachi, Thatta, Dhabeji aur Vinder ke log alag hain. Wahan K-Electric bijli deti hai. Ye kisi DISCO ka hissa nahi.

Is liye kuch baatein yahan badal jati hain aur pehli baat, aap ka number alag hai.

K-Electric ka account number 13 digits ka hota hai. DISCO walon ka reference number 14 digits ka. Ye farq yaad rakhein.

Doosri baat, bill ka raasta alag hai. Aap ko bill.pitc.com.pk wale raaste kaam nahi aayenge. Wahan sirf DISCO hain.

K-Electric ke bill ke liye un ki apni website hai. Wahan account number dal kar bill nikal lein.

Teesri baat, shikayat ka raasta. Helpline 118 K-Electric ke liye bhi hai. Ye un ki apni site par likha hai.

Chauthi baat, NEPRA tak jana ho to form mein KE wala option chunein. Wahan Existing, New aur KE teen hote hain.

Baqi sab kuch yahi hai. Meter test ho ya Electric Inspector. NEPRA ka manual K-Electric par bhi lagu hota hai.

Sawalat

Aksar poochhe gaye sawal

Bijli ka bill zyada aya to sab se pehle kya karein?

Meter par jayein. Wahan jo reading chal rahi hai, woh note karein. Usay bill ke 'Present Reading' se milayein. Farq nikal lein. Phir DISCO ko likhit shikayat dein.

Shikayat ke baad kitne din mein jawab aana chahiye?

NEPRA ki kitab ke mutabiq ghalat reading ki shikayat 7 din mein mukammal honi chahiye. Late payment surcharge ki ghalti 3 din mein aur detection bill ka faisla 15 din mein. Tariff ki shikayat 30 din mein.

Meter tez chal raha hai to kya karoon?

Pehle DISCO ko likh kar kahein ke mera meter theek se check karein. Meter challenge fee jama karwayein. Single phase ke liye 500 rupaye, three phase ke liye 1000. DISCO ko 7 working days mein test karna hoga. Meter tez nikla to kharcha DISCO ka, sath do cycles ka credit.

Detection bill kitne mahine ka aa sakta hai?

Registered consumer ke liye zyada se zyada chhe mahine. Unregistered ke liye barah mahine. Hisaab pichhli consumption se lagega, ya aane wale mahinon ke muqable se. Mann maani raqam nahi lag sakti.

NEPRA mein shikayat kaise darj karwayein?

NEPRA ke online Complaint Management System par jayein aur reference number, CNIC aur mobile number dalen. Pichhli DISCO shikayat ki copy lagayein. Affidavit dena hoga. Files pdf ya jpg mein hon, har ek 2.5 MB se chhoti.

Kya shikayat ke liye pehle DISCO jana zaroori hai?

Haan. NEPRA ka form poochhta hai ke aap ne DISCO ko shikayat ki thi ya nahi. NEPRA khud likhta hai ke pehle SDO ya XEN ke paas jayein. Bina is ke NEPRA shikayat aage nahi barhati.

NEPRA ka fon number kya hai?

051-2013200. Ye NEPRA head office aur DG Consumer Affairs dono ka number hai. Email hai cad@nepra.org.pk. Waqt subah 9 se sham 5, peer se jumma aur nEPRA ki 'Asaan Approach' mobile app bhi maujood hai.

Aakhri baat

Ab aap kar sakte hain

Aap ke paas ab chaar tareeqe hain. SMS aur phone call. Office. Ya kisi dukan se. Internet ki zaroorat kisi mein bhi nahi.

Sab se pehle apna reference number sambhal lein aur isay phone mein save kar lein. Phir SMS wala tareeqa aazmayein. Kaam na bane to 118 par call kar dein. Itna hi kaafi hai.
