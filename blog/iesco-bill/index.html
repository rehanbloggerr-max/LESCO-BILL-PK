Prepare

What you need

Before you check your bill, find your IESCO reference number or customer ID on any previous bill. That identifier is what the system uses to pull the correct consumer account.

If you manage multiple meters, double-check which account belongs to the house, shop, or office you want to view.

Tip: keep the latest paper bill nearby so you can compare the month, meter, and total payable amount with the online result.
Online check

How to check your IESCO bill online

BijleeBill helps you move directly toward the official IESCO billing route so you can view the current month’s bill, payable amount, and due date without digging through a printed copy.

  1. Open the IESCO bill optionUse the BijleeBill bill checker and select IESCO from the DISCO list.
  2. Enter your reference numberPaste the number exactly as printed on your bill.
  3. Submit the formWait a moment for the current bill details to load.

Duplicate copy

Download or print the duplicate IESCO bill

Once the online bill is visible, you can usually save it to your phone or computer and print it when needed. A duplicate copy is helpful if the paper bill is lost, delayed, or damaged.

  • Use the print option on the official bill page if available.
  • Save a PDF screenshot for your records.
  • Keep the transaction receipt after payment.
Note: a duplicate bill shows the same billing account details as the current issue, so verify the meter number and amount before paying.
Before paying

What to verify before paying your IESCO bill

Payment errors are usually avoidable. Before you approve a transaction, check that the bill amount, billing month, and reference number all match the current account you intend to pay.

  • Confirm the consumer name or account reference.
  • Check whether the amount matches the latest bill.
  • Look for arrears or previous balance entries.
  • Save the final confirmation receipt after payment.
Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting common problems

If the bill does not load, make sure the number is entered correctly and that the month has been updated on the official system. If the amount looks unusually high, compare the billed reading with your physical meter reading first.

Important: if the billed units do not match your meter, contact the official IESCO support channel and keep the old bills ready for reference.

Common fixes

  • Remove spaces or extra characters from the number.
  • Make sure you selected the correct DISCO.
  • Refresh the official portal if the page times out.
  • Try again after a few minutes during peak hours.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I check IESCO bill without the paper bill?

Yes, if you know the reference number or customer ID. That number is enough to retrieve the current bill.

Can I save the online bill as a PDF?

If the official page supports printing or saving, you can usually save it as a PDF from your browser.

Does BijleeBill store my IESCO number?

No. We redirect you to the official billing route and do not store the number you enter.
