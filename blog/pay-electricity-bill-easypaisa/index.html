Start here

The due date is tomorrow. The nearest bank branch is across town and the queue outside it will take an hour.

There is a faster way sitting in your phone right now. Easypaisa runs on every mobile network in Pakistan and pays electricity, gas, water, telephone, internet, solar and credit card bills.

Easypaisa describes the range on its own site as more than a hundred kinds of bills. Electricity sits right at the front of that list.

Most guides online will hand you eight steps and wish you luck. They tend to leave out the one thing deciding whether the payment lands at all.

That thing is the number you type in. Get it wrong and the money either bounces back or lands on somebody else's connection.

This page follows the steps easypaisa publishes on its own website. It also covers the two number formats, the fee question and what to do after money leaves your wallet.

Quick answer

The five official steps

Easypaisa publishes the whole flow on its FAQ page. Five steps. They do not change.

Log in to your account. Tap on Bill Payments. Select the bill you want to pay.

Choose your company from the drop-down menu. Add the number the field asks for, then pay.

That is the entire process. Everything below is detail, safety and the few places people get stuck.

One caution before you start. Easypaisa shows you a name and an amount on the confirmation screen.

Read both. A payment against the wrong connection is far harder to undo than a payment made five minutes late.

What you need

Three things before you open the app

Get these three ready first. The payment itself then takes under two minutes.

Your number. Fourteen digits for every distribution company and thirteen for K-Electric. The next section covers both in detail.

Enough balance. The wallet needs the full bill amount sitting in it before you start. Top up from any bank account or any debit or credit card, from inside the app.

A working account. Sign-up needs a valid CNIC and a mobile number in your own name. No other documentation is asked for.

There is no charge for opening the account. Every network works. Jazz, Zong, Ufone and Telenor are all fine.

Keep your bill in front of you as well. You will want to compare the amount on screen with the amount printed on paper.

Your number

Which number to type, and why guides get it wrong

Here is the single most useful thing on this page. The number you type depends entirely on who bills you.

Who bills youNumber to enterWhere it sits on the bill
Any DISCO, all ten of them14 digit reference numberTop left, in the consumer details box. Three groups of digits with a letter at the end, for example 04 11523 0012345 U
K-Electric, Karachi13 digit account numberTop of the bill beside Account No. Example format 0400012345678

K-Electric answers this itself on its Know Your Bill page. The account number is the single most important reference for customers and is required for every interaction.

K-Electric also addresses the other two numbers on that page. The contract number and the consumer number are for internal use by K-Electric and its vendors.

So the eight digit consumer number printed on a Karachi bill will not fetch your bill. Guides asking for it are describing other companies.

For everyone else the reference number is fourteen digits. Type the digits only, with no spaces and no letter at the end.

That number never changes from month to month. Save it in your phone contacts once and you are done with this step forever.

Set up Easypaisa

Opening an account, with or without a smartphone

Already have an account? Skip ahead. Everyone else needs about three minutes.

Easypaisa publishes the sign-up steps on its FAQ page. They run in this order.

  1. Install the app. It is on the Play Store, the Apple App Store and the Huawei AppGallery. Scan the QR code on the easypaisa homepage to jump straight to the right store.
  2. Enter your mobile number and tap Next. The account is tied to this number.
  3. Enter your CNIC number and its date of issuance. Both are printed on the card. The date matters. Copy it exactly.
  4. Let the OTP arrive. The app fetches it automatically from your SIM. Do not switch networks at this point.
  5. Accept the terms and conditions after reading them.
  6. Create a five digit PIN. This authorises every payment you make from now on. Pick one you will remember. Nothing else asks for it.

No smartphone? Easypaisa runs on USSD as well. You do not need mobile data for it.

Telenor customers dial *786#. Zong, Ufone and Jazz customers dial *2262#. Then enter your CNIC and its date of issuance. Set a five digit PIN.

One CNIC holds one account. One number holds one account. The name on the account comes from the CNIC and cannot be changed later.

An expired CNIC will not open an account. Renew it first, then come back to this step.

Trouble with an old account on your CNIC? Call the easypaisa helpline for help with the migration. Telenor users dial 3737. Everyone else calls 042-111-003-737.

Pay step by step

How to pay electricity bill Easypaisa, screen by screen

Open the app and log in with your five digit PIN. Now follow the flow easypaisa publishes.

  1. Tap Bill Payments on the home screen. It sits alongside money transfer, mobile load and the other payment tiles.
  2. Select the bill type. Tap Electricity. Gas, water, telephone, internet, solar and credit card bills sit in the same menu.
  3. Choose your company from the drop-down menu. Every distribution company is listed here, along with K-Electric.
  4. Enter your number in the field shown. Fourteen digit reference number for a DISCO, thirteen digit account number for K-Electric. Digits only.
  5. Read the details the app fetches. Your name, the amount and the due date appear on screen. Check all three against your paper bill.
  6. Tap pay and confirm with your PIN. The amount leaves your wallet balance at once.

The app pulls the bill straight from the company's own system. You do not type the amount yourself. That removes the commonest source of error.

A confirmation screen follows with a transaction ID. Photograph it before you close the app.

Check the screen

Three checks before you tap pay

This takes ten seconds and prevents almost every bad outcome. Do it every single time.

Check the name. The app displays the consumer name registered against the number. It should be yours or somebody in your family.

A stranger's name means a wrong digit somewhere. Stop and go back. Retype the number rather than paying.

Check the amount. Compare the figure on screen with the payable amount printed on your paper bill.

A difference usually means you are looking at a newer bill than the paper one in your hand. Check the bill month shown on screen.

Check the due date. Easypaisa shows it as part of the fetched details. Paying on the last day leaves no room for a delay at the company's end.

All three match? Tap pay. The payment leaves your wallet at once and the receipt lands in your history.

Keep the proof

What to keep after the money leaves

A payment is only finished once you can prove it. Easypaisa gives you three things to hold on to.

The transaction ID. It appears on the confirmation screen and arrives by SMS. Write it down or photograph the screen.

The entry in your app history. Every payment stays listed inside the app under your transaction history. It works as a receipt on its own.

The confirmation SMS. It lands within moments and carries the transaction details with it.

Keep all three until you have seen the payment reflected on your next bill. Then you can stop worrying about it.

Next working day, check the bill online with the same reference number. A cleared payment shows the balance brought to zero.

Check once a month and you will never argue with a company about a payment again.

What it costs

Is there a fee to pay electricity bill Easypaisa

No. Easypaisa states it directly on its FAQ page.

The exact words on the official page say the service is free for customers. There is no per-transaction charge and no percentage.

Confusion comes from one part of the same answer. Easypaisa adds a note for agents. Users working as agents or deemed agents are levied charges on a sliding scale.

Count of billsService fee, inclusive of tax
6 to 10 bills per monthRs. 5 per month
More than 10 bills per monthRs. 10 per month

Those figures apply to agents running other people's payments through their own account. A customer paying their own electricity bill pays nothing.

Five bills or fewer in a month sit below the agent table altogether. Either way, paying your own bill costs you the bill amount and no more.

One other cost is worth mentioning. Topping up your wallet depends on the bank or card you move the money from.

When it fails

Three reasons a payment is refused, and the fix for each

Easypaisa lists the reasons a bill payment will not go through. There are only three.

  1. The number you entered is wrong. Check it digit by digit against your bill. Spaces and the trailing letter must be left out. K-Electric needs thirteen digits, not fourteen.
  2. Your balance is too low. The wallet needs the full amount before you start. Add funds from a bank account or card, then run the payment again.
  3. You have hit a limit. Daily, monthly and yearly limits all apply on every account. Upgrading your level raises all three at once.

The third one catches people out most. Limits are tied to your account level rather than to the bill itself.

Upgrading happens inside the app. Tap your profile picture, tap Account Level, then tap Upgrade your Account.

Easypaisa asks you to scan your left and right hand here. Fill in your details, then tap Upgrade. Biometric verification is part of the process.

A wrong PIN blocks the payment as well. Five wrong attempts are worth avoiding. Pause and breathe. Re-enter the digits one at a time.

Limits

Account levels, and why a payment is blocked

Easypaisa does not publish one flat limit for everybody. Limits move with your account level.

Three windows apply to every account: a daily limit, a monthly limit and a yearly limit. Sending and receiving each carry their own set.

An unverified account sits at the lowest level and carries the smallest limits. Most people paying a household electricity bill never come near them.

Problems start with larger commercial bills or with several bills paid in one month. That is the point to upgrade.

The upgrade path sits behind your profile picture in the app. Account Level, then Upgrade your Account, then the hand scan.

Once upgraded, the higher limits apply to every payment afterwards. You only do this once.

Money gone, bill not

When the wallet charged you and the bill still shows unpaid

This is the failure mode almost no guide covers. It is also the one causing the most panic.

Your balance dropped. The confirmation screen appeared. A day later the bill still shows the old amount.

Do not pay again. Paying twice turns a delay into a refund chase lasting far longer.

  1. Find the transaction ID first. It is in your app history and in the confirmation SMS. Note it down.
  2. Screenshot the history entry. It carries the date, the amount and the company name. This is your evidence.
  3. Wait one working day. Companies post payments in batches. A weekend or a public holiday slows the process down.
  4. Recheck the bill online with your reference number after that working day has passed.
  5. Call the easypaisa helpline with your transaction ID. Telenor users dial 3737. Other networks call 042-111-003-737.

No official page publishes a fixed clearing window. Treat the timing as unconfirmed. Judge it by what your own bill shows.

Keep the transaction ID until the payment appears on a bill. That single habit settles every dispute of this kind.

Paying in cash

Using an Easypaisa agent instead of the app

No account, no smartphone and no data? The agent route still works. It runs on cash.

Start by finding any shop displaying the easypaisa sign. Give the agent your number along with the cash. Ask them to pay it.

The agent enters the number in their own app. They read the amount back to you, take the cash and complete the payment.

Ask for the printed slip or for the confirmation SMS to be sent to your own mobile number. Either one is your receipt.

Check the agent types the number you said, not the number they think you meant. Read it back off their screen before they confirm.

The agent-side charges shown earlier apply to the agent's own account. Agree on any service fee before you hand over the cash.

Two of those matter much more than the rest. Never share your five digit PIN. Never let an agent log in on your behalf.

Easypaisa works only inside Pakistan. The account cannot be used from abroad.

Mistakes

Seven mistakes, and the one habit that prevents them

  1. Typing fourteen digits for K-Electric. Karachi uses a thirteen digit account number. The fourteen digit reference belongs to the other companies.
  2. Typing the consumer number instead. K-Electric states its contract and consumer numbers are internal only.
  3. Including the spaces or the letter. Enter the digits alone. Drop every space and the trailing U or R.
  4. Skipping the name check. The app shows the registered consumer name. Read it before you pay.
  5. Paying again after a delay. Wait one working day and recheck the bill. Never pay twice.
  6. Leaving the wallet empty. Top up before you start rather than halfway through the payment.
  7. Paying on the due date itself is asking for trouble. Give yourself two clear days instead. The surcharge is entirely avoidable.

Six of those seven take five seconds to avoid. The seventh costs one calendar reminder.

Save your number in your phone today. Every future payment then starts with a copy and paste.

Checklist

The full checklist in one place

  • Find your number: a 14 digit reference or a 13 digit account for K-Electric.
  • Save the number in your phone contacts once, digits only.
  • Install easypaisa from the Play Store, App Store or Huawei AppGallery.
  • Sign up with your CNIC and its date of issuance, then set a 5 digit PIN.
  • Top up the wallet so the full bill amount is sitting in it.
  • Tap Bill Payments, then Electricity, then pick your company.
  • Type the number. Digits only, no spaces, no trailing letter.
  • Read the consumer name on screen and confirm it is yours.
  • Match the amount and due date against your paper bill.
  • Pay, then photograph the confirmation screen with the transaction ID.
  • Check your in-app history for the entry. Keep the SMS with it.
  • Recheck the bill online the next working day to see it cleared.
  • Never pay twice. Wait one working day before you worry.
  • Call 3737 on Telenor or 042-111-003-737 on any other network.

Print this checklist or keep it in your phone. It takes the guesswork out of every call you make.

How do I pay electricity bill Easypaisa?

Log in, tap Bill Payments, select Electricity, choose your company from the drop-down menu, then enter your number. Fourteen digits for a DISCO, thirteen for K-Electric. Confirm the name and amount, then pay with your PIN.

Is there a fee for paying an electricity bill through Easypaisa?

No. Easypaisa states on its own FAQ page the service is free for customers. A separate sliding charge applies only to accounts working as agents, starting at Rs. 5 a month beyond five bills.

Which number do I enter for K-Electric?

Your 13 digit account number, printed at the top of your K-Electric bill. K-Electric states this on its own Know Your Bill page. The account number is the single most important reference for customers. The contract and consumer numbers are internal only.

Where do I find my 14 digit reference number?

Top left of the bill, inside the consumer details box. It prints in three groups with a letter at the end, for example 04 11523 0012345 U. Enter the digits only, with no spaces and no letter.

Why is my Easypaisa bill payment failing?

Easypaisa gives three reasons. Either the number entered is wrong or the balance is too low. Or a daily, monthly or yearly limit was reached. Upgrade your account level inside the app to raise the limits.
