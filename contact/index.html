Email us

The one address that reaches us

Write to support@bijleebill.pk for anything about this website. One address covers everything: corrections, feedback, bug reports and business enquiries.

Please mention the page or guide you are writing about, plus your company name (LESCO, IESCO, MEPCO and so on). It helps us answer in the first reply instead of the third.

What we can help with

Site questions, corrections and feedback

We handle questions about the bill checker, the calculator and every guide on the blog. If a number, a route or a step is wrong anywhere on the site, tell us and we will verify and fix it.

For advertising and partnership enquiries, the same email works. Mention AdSense or partnership in the subject line.

Official channels

Bill problems belong to your company

We cannot change bills, waive charges or resolve meter disputes, because we do not run the billing systems. Those tasks belong to your electricity company:

  • Helpline 118 for outages and quick complaints
  • CCMS portal (ccms.pitc.com.pk) for billing complaints with tracking
  • Your sub-division office for readings, corrections and detection bills
  • Your bank or wallet for payment disputes

Our guides explain each route step by step. Start from the blog index if you are unsure which one fits.

Response time

Two to three working days

We answer most emails within two to three working days, in English or Urdu. Urgent bill deadlines do not wait for email. For anything time-sensitive, please use helpline 118 first. Write to us afterwards.
