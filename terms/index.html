The service

A convenience layer, not a billing office

BijleeBill.pk routes you to the official bill pages of Pakistani electricity companies and explains the process in plain language. The bill figures always come from the company's own system.

Calculator outputs are estimates for guidance. Actual charges follow your company's tariff, taxes and adjustments. We work hard to keep every route and guide accurate, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted availability.
